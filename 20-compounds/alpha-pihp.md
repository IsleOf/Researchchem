# α-PiHP

**Category:** [[stimulants]]  
**Legal / approval status:** Controlled in EU/UK/US (as α-PHP isomer analog in many places).

## Summary
Pyrrolidinophenone cathinone; strong dopamine/noradrenaline reuptake blocker. Among the most-seized cathinones in Europe.

## Key risks
- Compulsive redosing
- Psychosis, agitation, hyperthermia
- Cardiac events
