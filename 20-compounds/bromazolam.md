# Bromazolam

**Category:** [[depressants]]  
**Legal / approval status:** Controlled in UK, US (temporary), many EU states.

## Summary
Dominant designer benzodiazepine in street samples 2024–26, often pressed as fake Xanax or mixed into opioids.

## Key risks
- Respiratory depression with opioids/alcohol
- Blackouts, amnesia
- Seizures on withdrawal
- Naloxone does not reverse benzos
