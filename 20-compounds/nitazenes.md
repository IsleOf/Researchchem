# Nitazenes (2-benzylbenzimidazole opioids)

**Category:** [[depressants]]  
**Legal / approval status:** Most controlled; new analogs keep appearing (22 recently cited by DEA).

## Summary
Ultra-potent synthetic opioids, mostly Chinese precursors/synthesis. EU notified 3 new nitazenes + 3 orphines in 2025.

## Key risks
- Many times stronger than fentanyl
- Need multiple naloxone doses
- Hidden in fake pills and other drugs
