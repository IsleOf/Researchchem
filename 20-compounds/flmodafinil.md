# Flmodafinil (CRL-40,940)

**Category:** [[wakefulness]]  
**Legal / approval status:** Unapproved. Named as a doping substance (2026).

## Summary
Bis-fluoro modafinil analog; longer wakefulness than modafinil in an animal comparison.

## Key risks
- No human clinical trials
- Insomnia, anxiety, headache
- Rare serious skin reactions known from modafinil class
- Banned in sport (WADA stimulant class)
