# ARA-290 — human evidence (L2)

Back to [[ara-290/index]]. All trials are small, short (≈28 days), phase 2, and sponsor-linked. Treat as **preliminary**.

| Study | Year | n | Design | Outcome | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Heij et al., Mol Med 18:1430-6 | 2012 | 22 | Double-blind placebo-controlled exploratory trial, IV 3×/week for 4 weeks, sarcoidosis with small-fibre neuropathy (SFN) | Improvement in neuropathic symptom/QoL measures reported; no safety concerns | https://pure.eur.nl/en/publications/ara-290-for-treatment-of-small-fiber-neuropathy-in-sarcoidosis/ |
| Dahan et al., Mol Med 19:334-45 | 2013 | small (≈22–40; exact n not verified) | Blinded placebo-controlled, 28 days SC, sarcoidosis SFN | Improved neuropathic symptoms, increased corneal small-nerve-fibre density, better 6MWT | https://doi.org/10.2119/molmed.2013.00122 |
| Brines et al., Mol Med 20:658-66 | 2015 | small phase 2 (exact n not verified) | RCT, 28 days SC + 28-day follow-up, type 2 diabetes with painful neuropathy | Improved PainDetect score; improvements in HbA1c and lipids reported within ARA-290 arm; corneal nerve density rose only in subgroup with low baseline | PMID 25387363 · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4365069 |
| Culver et al., Invest Ophthalmol Vis Sci 58(6) | 2017 | 64 | Phase 2b, 28-day RCT, sarcoidosis-associated small-fibre loss + neuropathic pain; primary endpoint corneal nerve fibre area (CCM) | Increased corneal nerve fibre area at the 4 mg arm vs placebo (sponsor: +23% from baseline); increased GAP-43+ regenerating intraepidermal fibres; symptom endpoints less clear | https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/publications/cibinetide-improves-corneal-nerve-fiber-abundance-in-patients-wit/ |

## Interpretation
- Signals are consistent across trials but rely on **surrogate** nerve-morphology endpoints and short follow-up; no durable clinical outcome or phase 3 data exist.
- No human data in healthy people, injury recovery, or performance.
