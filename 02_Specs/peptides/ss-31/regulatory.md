# SS-31 — regulatory (L2)

Back to [[ss-31/index]].

| Region / body | Status |
|---|---|
| **US FDA** | **Accelerated approval, 19 Sep 2025 — Forzinity (elamipretide HCl) injection**, Stealth BioTherapeutics, to improve muscle strength in adult and pediatric Barth syndrome patients ≥30 kg. Based on knee-extensor strength (intermediate endpoint) from TAZPOWER; continued approval contingent on a confirmatory randomised trial. Preceded by a Complete Response Letter (May 2025). Sponsor received a rare pediatric disease priority review voucher. Label URL cited as accessdata.fda.gov 215244s000lbl.pdf (NDA number from URL; not independently verified). |
| EU (EMA) | Not authorised (no EU marketing authorisation found). |
| Other indications | PMM, AMD/GA, heart failure: investigational only. |
| Confirmatory trial | 4TAZPOWER phase 3b/4 (ex-US, ~48 males) registered (UK HRA summary). |
| **WADA** | Not named on the Prohibited List (not verified against the official current List). Before 2025 it would have fallen under S0 (non-approved substances); post-approval S0 status is unclear — athletes should check with their anti-doping organisation. |
