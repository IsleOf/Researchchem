# SS-31 — risks (L2)

Back to [[ss-31/index]].

## Observed in trials / label
- **Injection-site reactions** — the most common adverse reaction (TAZPOWER, MMPOWER-3, ReCLAIM-2; Forzinity label). Usually mild–moderate (`risk:injection-site-reaction`).
- ReCLAIM-2: any AE 86% elamipretide vs 71% placebo, mostly injection-site.
- MMPOWER-3: most AEs mild–moderate; no major safety signal.
- **Benzyl alcohol** excipient in Forzinity → not approved for neonates (gasping syndrome warning).
- **Renal impairment**: insufficient information for eGFR <30 mL/min or dialysis.

## Not observed / not expected
- No hormone axis suppression reported (not a hormonal agent).

## Unknowns & harm-reduction notes
- Long-term safety data come from small rare-disease cohorts; confirmatory trial pending.
- Grey-market "SS-31" products are unregulated: identity, purity, sterility and endotoxin are unverified; peptide counterfeits and contamination are a real risk independent of the molecule itself.
- No data supporting use in healthy people; benefit–risk outside Barth syndrome is unestablished.
