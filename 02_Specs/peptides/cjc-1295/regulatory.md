# CJC-1295 — regulatory status

## Approval
**Not approved as a medicine in any region.** CJC-1295 never completed phase 2.
ConjuChem's development programme ended in 2006 (see [[cjc-1295/risks]]). There is no
marketing authorization in the US, EU, UK, Canada, Australia or Japan, and no
USP/NF monograph for the substance.

## United States — compounding (503A/503B)

CJC-1295 is **not a component of any FDA-approved drug**, so it cannot be compounded on
that basis. Its route to legal compounding would be inclusion on the **503A Bulk Drug
Substances List**, and that has not happened.

Timeline, as far as it can be established from FDA material:
- FDA placed CJC-1295 in **Category 2** of its interim list of bulk drug substances
  nominated for compounding — i.e. substances FDA had enough information to evaluate and
  which it judged to **raise significant safety risks**. FDA's safety-risk material
  identified serious adverse events associated with CJC-1295 including increased heart
  rate and a systemic vasodilatory reaction, and stated that available clinical data are
  limited.
- In **2024** FDA recorded that CJC-1295 was removed from Category 2 **because the
  nominations were withdrawn by the nominators** — a procedural removal, not a safety
  clearance.
- FDA then brought CJC-1295-related substances to its **Pharmacy Compounding Advisory
  Committee (PCAC) on 4 December 2024**. FDA's briefing questions for that meeting
  **proposed that CJC-1295 (free base), CJC-1295 acetate and CJC-1295 DAC *not* be added
  to the 503A Bulks List.**

**Net position:** CJC-1295 is not on the 503A bulks list, FDA has formally proposed against
adding it, and FDA has documented significant safety concerns. Reported PCAC vote tallies
circulating on commercial peptide sites could not be verified against FDA's own meeting
record and are **not** reproduced here. PCAC votes are advisory and rulemaking has not
concluded.

## Anti-doping — WADA

**Prohibited at all times, Section S2** (Peptide Hormones, Growth Factors, Related
Substances and Mimetics). CJC-1295 is named explicitly in the **GHRH analogues** sub-group
of S2, alongside CJC-1293, sermorelin and tesamorelin — it appears by name in official WADA
Prohibited List editions (e.g. 2017, 2020). The companion sub-group of **growth hormone
secretagogues** names anamorelin, [[ipamorelin]], macimorelin and tabimorelin.

Practically: both halves of the commonly sold CJC-1295 + ipamorelin blend are S2-prohibited,
and S2 substances are banned in and out of competition. Athletes should check the current
edition at https://www.wada-ama.org/en/prohibited-list — the specific section numbering has
shifted between editions, and this note is based on official 2017–2020 editions rather than
the current year's text.

## Other regions
No approval anywhere. In most jurisdictions an unapproved peptide of this kind is an
unlicensed medicinal product: lawful to hold as a laboratory reference material, unlawful to
supply or market for human use. "Research use only" labelling is a supply-side device, not a
legal category that makes human use lawful.
