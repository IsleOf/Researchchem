# Ipamorelin — human evidence

## Phase 2 postoperative ileus RCT — negative (the key human result)
- **Design:** prospective, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled **phase 2
  proof-of-concept** trial. Registered as **NCT00672074**; sponsor Helsinn Therapeutics (US).
- **Population:** n = 117 enrolled. Adults undergoing small or large bowel resection,
  open or laparoscopic. Intravenous ipamorelin or placebo twice daily from postoperative
  day 1 to day 7 or discharge.
- **Primary-type outcome:** **missed.** Median time to first tolerated meal 25.3 h with
  ipamorelin vs 32.6 h with placebo, **p = 0.15**. Efficacy analyses showed no significant
  differences.
- **Safety:** treatment-emergent adverse events in 87.5% of the ipamorelin group vs 94.8%
  of placebo — i.e. no excess AE signal versus placebo in this short IV-exposure setting.
- **Authors' limitation:** small study, patients with a broad range of underlying conditions.
- *Int J Colorectal Dis* 2014;29(12):1527-34. PMID 25331030. doi:10.1007/s00384-014-2030-8.
  Registry: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00672074

**Why this matters beyond ileus:** this is the only adequately designed, placebo-controlled
clinical trial of ipamorelin for any indication whose results have been published, and it
was negative on efficacy. The directional trend (25.3 vs 32.6 h) was in the hypothesized
direction but did not reach significance.

## NCT01280344 — larger phase 2, completed, results never published
- **Design:** Helsinn-sponsored phase 2 in patients recovering from bowel resection;
  three ipamorelin arms and placebo. Primary outcome: recovery of GI function within 10 days.
- **Enrollment:** 320 (actual). Start April 2011; primary completion June 2013;
  final completion May 2014. Registry status **completed**.
- **Outcome:** **no results summary posted and no publication identified.** Development of
  ipamorelin for this indication did not continue.
- **✅ Verified against the registry record itself, 2026-11-09** (ClinicalTrials.gov API v2):
  overall status `COMPLETED`; enrolment **320, type ACTUAL**; start 2011-04 (actual),
  primary completion 2013-06 (actual), completion 2014-05 (actual); **`hasResults: false`**
  with **no results-first-posted date** — so the claim that results were never posted is
  confirmed on the registry, not merely absent from the literature. Four arms are
  registered (three ipamorelin arms and a matching placebo comparator), sponsor
  **Helsinn Therapeutics (U.S.), Inc**, primary outcome "Recovery of Gastrointestinal (GI)
  Function" up to 10 days. Note that as a completed industry trial this is also a
  **results-reporting gap**, not only a publication gap.
- **Interpretation:** an unpublished completed trial of this size is itself an evidential
  fact. It should be read as a likely further negative, but it is formally *unknown*, and
  it is a substantial publication-bias hole in the ipamorelin literature.
- Registry: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01280344

## Gobburu 1999 — human PK/PD
- **Design:** dose-escalation PK/PD study, intravenous ipamorelin, **8 healthy male
  subjects per dose level**.
- **Outcome:** terminal half-life ~2 h; clearance 0.078 L/h/kg; Vss 0.22 L/kg;
  dose-proportional; GH release peaked ~0.67 h post-dose; indirect response model fitted;
  inter-individual PD variability exceeded PK variability.
- **Scope:** establishes that ipamorelin releases GH in humans and how fast. No clinical
  endpoint.
- *Pharm Res* 1999;16(9):1412-6. PMID 10496658

## What does not exist in humans

Stated explicitly because the gap is the finding:
- **No trial of lean mass, fat mass, body composition, strength, or physical function.**
- **No trial of sleep, recovery, injury healing, joint pain, or skin.**
- **No trial in ageing, frailty, sarcopenia, or cachexia.**
- **No long-term safety or exposure data.** The longest published human exposure is ~7 days
  of IV dosing in postoperative patients.
- **No trial of ipamorelin combined with [[cjc-1295]]**, the form in which it is most
  commonly sold.
- **No phase 3 trial, for any indication.**

## Related class evidence (not ipamorelin)
For the one long-term randomized dataset on any GH secretagogue, see MK-677 in
[[ipamorelin/risks]] (Nass 2008, PMID 18981485): 12 months of a ghrelin mimetic increased
fat-free mass but did **not** reduce intra-abdominal adiposity, and reduced insulin
sensitivity. This is the closest thing to evidence for the body-composition claim made for
ipamorelin, and it is a different molecule with a different (oral, non-peptide,
long-acting) profile.
