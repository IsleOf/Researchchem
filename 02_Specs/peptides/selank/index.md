# Selank

**What it is:** A synthetic heptapeptide, Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg-Pro-Gly-Pro: the immunomodulatory tetrapeptide **tuftsin** (an IgG fragment) extended with a stabilising C-terminal Pro-Gly-Pro. Developed at the Institute of Molecular Genetics, RAS (Moscow), and marketed in Russia as an intranasal anxiolytic. Member of [[nootropic-regulatory-peptides]].

**Status:** Registered in Russia as an anxiolytic. Secondary sources say this was in 2009, for generalised anxiety disorder and neurasthenia; the official register was not checked directly. Not approved by FDA, EMA or other Western regulators. See [[selank/regulatory]].

**Evidence tier: 3.** The best human evidence is one Russian trial in 62 patients, randomised against the benzodiazepine medazepam rather than placebo. Mechanistic work comes mainly from the same institute.

## Key points
1. **Human anxiety evidence is a single active-comparator trial:** n=62, GAD/neurasthenia, Selank vs medazepam ([PMID 18454096](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18454096/)). There is no placebo-controlled RCT, and the "38 % vs 20 % HAM-A vs placebo" figures quoted online could not be traced.
2. **Mechanism is multi-pronged and low-potency:** positive allosteric modulation of [3H]GABA binding ([doi:10.2174/0929866525666180925144642](https://doi.org/10.2174/0929866525666180925144642)), changes in GABAergic gene expression in rat cortex ([Volkova 2016](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphar.2016.00031)), and inhibition of enkephalin-degrading enzymes (IC50 ≈ 20 µM, [PMID 11443939](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11443939/)).
3. **Anxiolysis in animals depends on strain:** effective in high-anxiety BALB/c mice but not in C57BL/6 mice. Opioid involvement is suggested by naloxone sensitivity.
