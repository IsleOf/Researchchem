# Selank: mechanism (pharmacology only)

## Structure
Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg-Pro-Gly-Pro, i.e. tuftsin (Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg) plus Pro-Gly-Pro for stability. Tuftsin is a natural immunomodulatory peptide that stimulates phagocytes.

## Proposed targets and pathways
| Target / pathway | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **GABA-A receptor, allosteric (`target:gabaa`)**: positive allosteric modulation of [3H]GABA binding; the effect combined with benzodiazepines is "not cumulative"; the authors propose subtype-selective modulation | Vyunova et al. 2018, Protein Pept Lett 25(10):914-923 ([doi:10.2174/0929866525666180925144642](https://doi.org/10.2174/0929866525666180925144642)) | Radioligand binding in rodent brain membranes; no direct high-affinity binding site shown |
| **GABAergic gene expression**: intranasal Selank changed mRNA of 45 of 84 neurotransmission genes at 1 h (22 at 3 h) in rat frontal cortex, and the changes correlated with those after GABA | Volkova et al. 2016, Front Pharmacol ([doi:10.3389/fphar.2016.00031](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphar.2016.00031), [PMC4757669](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4757669/)) | Rat, mRNA only |
| Same, in a neuroblastoma cell line: Selank given with GABA almost completely suppressed GABA-induced gene changes | IMR-32 cells, Front Pharmacol 2017 ([PMID 28293190](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28293190/)) | Cell line |
| **Enkephalin-degrading enzymes (`target:enkephalinase`)**: inhibits human-serum enkephalinases, IC50 ≈ 20 µM. In BALB/c mice it prolongs plasma leu-enkephalin half-life, and naloxone attenuates its effect | Kost et al. 2001 ([PMID 11443939](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11443939/)); "inhibitory effect of selank on enkephalin degrading enzymes as a possible mechanism of its anxiolytic activity" ([PMID 11550013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11550013/)) | In vitro + mouse; µM potency |
| **Immunomodulation (tuftsin lineage)**: changes cytokine and chemokine gene expression (mouse spleen) and IL-6 gene expression in human blood cells. The **direction of the IL-6 effect is inconsistent** across reports | Uchakina et al. 2008 ([PMID 18577961](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18577961/)); [RUDN record](https://repository.rudn.ru/en/records/article/record/156052) | Ex vivo / mouse |
| **Monoamines**: lowered hippocampal 5-HT and 5-HIAA in BALB/c but not C57BL/6 mice (2008) | Secondary summary; primary not verified | Weak |

## Kinetics (no dosing)
- A review by Koroleva & Myasoedov reports that Selank's half-life in blood is **only a few minutes**, like that of enkephalins. They take this as indirect support that it acts as a substrate/competitive inhibitor of enkephalinases ([doi:10.1134/S1062359019040071](https://doi.org/10.1134/S1062359019040071)).
- In rats, peak brain content after intranasal delivery was ~0.16 % of the administered amount (review of intranasal neuropeptide delivery, [journals.rcsi.science](https://journals.rcsi.science/0091-150X/article/view/245621)).
- No human pharmacokinetic study was found.
