# Pinealon: risks

Up: [[pinealon/index]]

> **Not found:** Human safety, toxicology, PK or interaction studies. Risk is **unknown**, not "low".

## Theoretical
- **`risk:no-human-safety-data`:** Nothing is known about adverse-event rates, pregnancy or interactions.
- **Proliferation / ERK activation:**
  - In vitro data describe activation of proliferative processes and altered cell cycle (PMID 21978084).
  - Long-term relevance to neoplasia is unknown and unstudied.
- **Serotonergic modulation:**
  - One report claims increased serotonin expression.
  - Interaction with SSRIs, MAOIs or triptans is theoretically conceivable but entirely unstudied.
- **`risk:unregulated-product-quality`:** Grey-market "research peptide" products have unverified identity, purity, sterility and endotoxin content. Intranasal and injectable products add route-specific risks.

## Hormonal
- No data on pineal, melatonin or HPG-axis effects in humans.

## Precautionary contraindications
- Pregnancy and breastfeeding.
- Neurological or psychiatric illness under treatment, where self-experimenting may delay effective care.
- Competitive athletes (see [[pinealon/regulatory]]).
