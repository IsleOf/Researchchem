# Pinealon: claims vs evidence

Up: [[pinealon/index]]

| Marketing claim | What the evidence shows | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "Boosts memory and cognition" | Rat offspring learning improved in a prenatal hyperhomocysteinaemia model (PMID 22567179). No human cognition data verified. | **Unsupported in humans** |
| "Neuroprotective / protects against Alzheimer's" | Spine preservation in cultures (PMID 28853087) and 5xFAD mice (2021). No human AD data. | **Preclinical only** |
| "Clinically proven for brain-injury recovery" | The 72-patient trial cited by vendors could not be located in indexed literature | **Unverified** |
| "Works epigenetically by binding DNA" | Docking and hypothesis papers from the developer group | **Speculative** |
| "Antioxidant" | Consistent in vitro ROS reductions (PMID 21978084) | **Supported in vitro only** |
| "Safe, no side effects" | No safety studies exist | **Unsupported** |
| "Activates the pineal gland / improves sleep" | No pineal or melatonin study of Pinealon found. The name refers to its origin in pineal-peptide research. | **Unsupported** |

**Quality flag:** All primary data come from the Khavinson group and collaborators, with no independent replication.
