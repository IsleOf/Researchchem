# Pinealon: human evidence

Up: [[pinealon/index]]

> **No indexed, controlled human trial of Pinealon was found.**

| Year | n | Design | Reported outcome | Link | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ~2015 (claimed) | 72 (claimed) | "Post-traumatic cerebrasthenia" trial, reportedly vs controls | Improved memory, less headache, better emotional stability (claimed) | Cited only on vendor/secondary sites | **Unverified.** No primary record located; not cited here as evidence. |
| unknown (claimed) | 32 (claimed) | Open-label cohort of polymorbid patients | "Improved adaptive capacity", slowed "biological age" markers (claimed) | Secondary sites only | **Unverified;** open-label, no placebo. |

## Assessment
- Human efficacy and safety are **unestablished**.
- If Russian-language originals exist, they are from the developer group and appear uncontrolled.
- Post-TBI asthenia studies that turn up in searches (phenotropil, enerion, Cerebrolysin) are **not** Pinealon studies. Do not conflate them.
