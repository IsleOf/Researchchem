## Review
- Khavinson, Linkova, Kozhevnikova, Trofimova, "EDR Peptide: Possible Mechanism…Alzheimer's Disease", *Molecules* 2021;26(1):159 ([PMC7795577](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7795577)). The review is developer-authored and mechanism-hypothesis focused.

## Assessment
- Signals are internally consistent but come from a small number of studies by one network.
- No independent replication was found.
- No dose-response, toxicology or behavioural replication in disease models was found.
