# Prostamax (KEDP)

**Family:** [[khavinson-bioregulators]]

## Summary
Prostamax is a synthetic tetrapeptide, Lys-Glu-Asp-Pro (KEDP). Khavinson-group sources present it as a prostate-targeted bioregulator for chronic prostatitis and "prostate ageing".

## Status at a glance
- **Approval:** Not approved by any stringent regulator.
- **FDA compounding record:** None found.
- **WADA:** Likely S0. See [[prostamax/regulatory]].

## Evidence tier: 3 (preclinical, extremely thin)
- **Prostatitis:** One rat chronic aseptic prostatitis study (2013).
- **Chromatin:** A few chromatin studies in lymphocytes from elderly donors (2004, 2012).
- **Human data:** No human clinical study was found.
- **Independence:** No independent replication was found.
