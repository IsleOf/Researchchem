# Semax: risks

**Overall:** the published Russian trials report few adverse events, but their safety reporting is thin, they are small and short, and there are no long-term data. **Absence of reported harm is not evidence of safety.**

| Risk | Evidence | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `risk:nasal-irritation`: mild irritation of the nasal mucosa with prolonged intranasal use | Russian product label as summarised by secondary sources | Low quality, but consistent |
| `risk:overstimulation`: headache, irritability, sleep disturbance | Secondary/anecdotal reports only | Not quantified in any primary study found |
| `risk:anxiogenic`: one 1996 report noted an anxiogenic component in behaviour | Secondary mention only | Unverified |
| `risk:immunogenicity`: cited (with impurity risk) as a reason for FDA's Category 2 placement | FDA 503A categories list ([fda.gov/media/94155](https://fda.gov/media/94155/download)); third-party summaries | Theoretical; no human data |
| `risk:unregulated-product`: grey-market "research" vials, with unknown identity, purity, sterility and endotoxin | General; underlies FDA's concern | The highest practical risk for non-Russian users |
| `risk:unknown-long-term` | No long-term studies found | Neurotrophic and immune gene modulation has unknown long-term consequences |

## Contraindications / special populations
- The Russian label (as summarised secondarily) excludes young children and lists no studies in under-18s for some indications. Pregnancy and lactation data were **not found**.
- The full official contraindication list was **not verified**. Check the Russian label (Vidal / GRLS).

## Interactions
None studied formally. It inhibits serum enkephalinase in vitro (IC50 ≈ 10 µM, [PMID 11443939](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11443939/)), so theoretical overlap with opioid-acting drugs is possible but unstudied.

## Hormone axis
Semax lacks the steroidogenic ACTH sequence. **No evidence was found** that it causes HPA-axis suppression or HPG suppression, but this has not been formally studied.
