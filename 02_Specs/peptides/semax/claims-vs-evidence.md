# Semax: claims vs evidence

| Marketing claim | What the evidence shows | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "Boosts BDNF / grows brain cells" | ↑ BDNF/TrkB mRNA and protein in **rat** hippocampus ([PMID 16996037](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16996037/)). One human stroke study reports higher plasma BDNF ([PMID 29798983](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29798983/)). Plasma BDNF does not show brain neurogenesis. | **Partly supported (animal); human relevance unproven** |
| "Clinically proven nootropic for healthy people" | Only tiny EEG/fMRI studies with imaging readouts (n=24, [PMID 30225715](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30225715/)). No RCT with cognitive endpoints in healthy adults was found. | **Not supported** |
| "Proven stroke treatment" | Approved for stroke in Russia, but the key study was non-randomised (30 vs 80, [PMID 11517472](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11517472/)). No hard-endpoint RCT and no independent replication. | **Weak / unproven** |
| "Treats ADHD, depression, anxiety" | No primary human trials located | **No evidence found** |
| "Neuroprotective after ischemia" | Consistent rat transcriptomic data ([PMID 24661604](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24661604/), [PMID 28255762](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28255762/)); functional infarct outcomes are less clear | **Plausible preclinically; unproven in humans** |
| "No side effects" | Small, short trials with minimal safety reporting; FDA flagged significant safety risk for compounding | **Not supported (unknown ≠ safe)** |
| "Legal / WADA-safe" | Not named by WADA, but S0 status is ambiguous; not approved in the US/EU | **Misleading** |
