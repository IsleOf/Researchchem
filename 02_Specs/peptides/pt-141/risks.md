# PT-141 — risks (L2)

Back to [[pt-141/index]]. Source: Vyleesi prescribing information (Drugs.com/NDC list reproductions) unless noted.

## Common adverse effects
- **Nausea** (`risk:nausea`) — the most common AE; manufacturer materials report ~40% in trials, with ~13% needing antiemetics; nausea was the leading cause of discontinuation (~8%).
- Flushing (`risk:flushing`), injection-site reactions, headache, vomiting.

## Cardiovascular
- **Transient blood-pressure increase and heart-rate decrease** after each dose (`risk:transient-hypertension`); label reports maximal increases of ~6 mmHg systolic / 3 mmHg diastolic; in an 8-day daily-dosing ambulatory BP study (n=127) mean daytime systolic rose 1.9 mmHg. Effects are transient (exact return-to-baseline time not verified here).
- **Contraindicated** in uncontrolled hypertension or known cardiovascular disease.

## Pigmentation
- **Focal hyperpigmentation** (`risk:hyperpigmentation`): 38% developed it with 8 consecutive daily doses in a dedicated study; more frequent in darker skin; **not always reversible** after stopping. Label advises against exceeding the labelled monthly frequency. (MC1R effect — same mechanism as Melanotan II tanning.)

## Interactions
- **Oral naltrexone**: bremelanotide can markedly reduce naltrexone exposure → risk of treatment failure in alcohol/opioid use disorder (`risk:naltrexone-interaction`). Avoid combination.
- Label reportedly notes possible slowing of gastric emptying affecting oral drugs that need rapid/threshold concentrations (specific drug list not verified here).

## Hormonal
- No HPG-axis suppression reported; not a hormonal agent.

## Harm-reduction notes
- Grey-market "PT-141" (including nasal sprays, which the original program abandoned partly over BP effects) has unverified identity/dose/sterility.
- Melanotan II, often co-used or confused with PT-141, is unapproved and has been linked in case reports to changes in moles/naevi (case-report level; not cited here); avoid conflating the two.
- Pregnancy: human data limited; consult label (not verified in detail here).
