# Epitalon (AEDG)

**Family:** [[khavinson-bioregulators]]

## Summary
Epitalon is a synthetic tetrapeptide (Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly). The St Petersburg Institute of Bioregulation and Gerontology (V. Khavinson's group) developed it as an analogue of **Epithalamin**, a polypeptide extract of bovine pineal gland. It is promoted as a "telomerase activator" and anti-ageing / sleep peptide.

## Status at a glance
- **Approval:** No stringent regulator (FDA, EMA, MHRA, TGA) has approved it for any indication. In Russia, Epithalamin (the extract) has had clinical use. Secondary sources say Epitalon is not a registered standalone medicine there; that is unverified. See [[epitalon/regulatory]].
- **US compounding:** FDA had placed it in Category 2 ("insufficient safety information"). In 2026 FDA said it would remove it from Category 2. The FDA advisory committee (PCAC) voted 7–4 on 24 Jul 2026 to add it to the 503A bulks list. FDA staff had recommended against, and formal rulemaking is still pending.
- **WADA:** Not named on the list; it is likely caught by S0 (non-approved substances). Unverified against the official list.

## Evidence tier: 3 (preclinical-dominant; human data weak)
- **Tier key used in this family:** 1 = replicated RCTs. 2 = some controlled human data. 3 = mostly preclinical or uncontrolled human data.
- **Cell work:** Telomerase and telomere data are in vitro. They are now partly replicated outside the Khavinson group (Al-Dulaimi 2025).
- **Human mortality and melatonin data:** These are mostly for **Epithalamin**, not Epitalon. They come from the developer group, in non-blinded or poorly described designs.
- **Quality concern:** Nearly all of the literature comes from one group (Khavinson / Anisimov, St Petersburg), often in Russian-language or group-affiliated journals.
