# Epitalon: human evidence

Up: [[epitalon/index]] · see also [[epitalon/claims-vs-evidence]]

> **Critical caveat:** Most "human Epitalon data" cited online are actually studies of **Epithalamin**, the bovine pineal extract. Epithalamin is a different, poorly defined product, and those results cannot be transferred to synthetic AEDG. Every study below is from the developer group. No independent, preregistered, placebo-controlled trial of Epitalon was found. No human trial has measured telomere length before and after Epitalon in a controlled design.

## Assessment
- **Epitalon itself:** The human evidence is essentially anecdotal or uncontrolled.
- **Epithalamin:** The mortality claims rest on small, developer-run studies with unclear randomisation. They have never been replicated.
- **Not found:** Registered trials (ClinicalTrials.gov-style) and independent human pharmacology or safety studies. Treat human efficacy as **unestablished**.
