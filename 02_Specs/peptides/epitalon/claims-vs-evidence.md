# Epitalon: claims vs evidence

Up: [[epitalon/index]] · data: [[epitalon/human-evidence]], [[epitalon/animal-evidence]]

| Marketing claim | What the evidence shows | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "Activates telomerase and lengthens telomeres" | True in cultured human cells (Khavinson 2003; independently replicated in vitro by Al-Dulaimi 2025). No human in vivo telomere data. | **Partly supported (in vitro only)** |
| "Reverses ageing / extends human lifespan" | Mouse data: max lifespan ~+12%, mean unchanged (Anisimov 2003). Human mortality data are for Epithalamin (the extract), from non-independent, poorly blinded studies. | **Unsupported for Epitalon in humans** |
| "Restores melatonin and fixes sleep" | Small uncontrolled Epithalamin studies show melatonin changes in the elderly. The Araj 2025 review cites a finding that Epitalon did not alter pineal melatonin secretion. No sleep RCT. | **Weak / conflicting** |
| "Prevents cancer" | Some rodent tumour reductions (leukaemia in SHR mice; HER-2/neu). Cancer cell lines show telomere extension via ALT. | **Unsupported; plausible risk in the opposite direction** |
| "Improves vision / retinitis pigmentosa" | A rat model plus an uncontrolled "90% positive" human claim | **Very weak** |
| "Proven safe, decades of Russian use" | No formal human safety, PK or pharmacovigilance data. Russian clinical use refers mostly to the extract. | **Unsupported** |
| "FDA-approved for compounding" | A PCAC advisory vote in favour (Jul 2026). Rulemaking pending; not approved as a drug. | **False / premature** |

**Quality flag:** Almost all supportive data originate from the Khavinson/Anisimov St Petersburg network, which has a commercial and institutional interest in these peptides. Weight them accordingly.
