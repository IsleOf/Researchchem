## Review
- Araj et al. 2025, *Int J Mol Sci* 26:2691, narrative review of in silico, in vitro and in vivo data ([PMC11943447](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11943447/)).
- No systematic review or meta-analysis was found.

## Assessment
- **Consistent signal:** Cell-culture telomerase and telomere effects, now with one independent replication.
- **Rodent lifespan and tumour data:** Modest and mixed, mostly from the Anisimov/Khavinson network.
- **Translation:** Nothing establishes telomerase activation in adult human tissue in vivo.
