# Psychedelics & phenethylamines

Substituted phenethylamines and tryptamines sold as psychedelics. **Added 2026-11-09** — previously a gap in this repo despite being one of the largest NPS families.

The defining risk in this class is **not potency in isolation — it is mis-selling**. The dangerous compounds are mostly encountered by people who believe they bought something else (usually LSD).

## Why this class sits apart from the rest of the repo
Most of this repo tracks substitution driven by *bans*. In this class the dominant dynamic is **substitution driven by counterfeiting**: an extremely potent compound is sold on blotter as a far less potent one. The harm comes from the gap between the two, not from either compound's own pharmacology.

## Current position (2026)
- **EUDA:** NPS still appearing at roughly **one a week**; 50 new in 2025, 1,050 monitored in total.
- **Netherlands, 14-year poisoning analysis (2012–2025):** cathinones, **phenethylamines** and benzodiazepines together accounted for **83% of all NPS poisonings** — so this class is a top-three contributor to actual harm presentations, not a fringe category. ([Addiction 2026](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/add.70511))
- The same analysis reports a **relative increase** for 2C-B, ketamine and LSD. ⚠️ Context and period of that figure **unverified** — the retrieved extract was truncated.
- **NIST RaDAR, Aug 2026:** nine compounds detected for the first time that month, including **25B-NBOH** (single West Coast sample) and **2C-C** (several West Coast samples). First-time detection of an NBOH is the signal worth watching here.
- **UNODC:** the **2C series is the largest** of these sub-families, and reported members keep increasing. UNODC also cautions that **detection counts do not indicate market share**, and that a proliferation of pharmacologically similar substances may signal supplier diversification rather than growing use.
- **Prodrugs appearing:** 2C-B-AN is documented as a 2C-B prodrug, with a Jan 2026 DEA reference. ⚠️ **Unverified.** Prodrugs matter because they sit outside parent-compound control language.

## Cross-cutting risks
- **Not detected by routine urine drug screens** — neither NBOMes nor LSD appear on standard panels, so a negative screen means nothing.
- **Serotonergic toxicity** rather than the classical LSD profile: tachycardia and hypertension dominate the recorded toxidrome; presentations can resemble serotonin syndrome.
- **Vasoconstriction** (DOx and NBOMe groups) — a mechanism absent from LSD and psilocybin.
- **Long duration** (DOx group) — can mean 12–24+ hours, which complicates emergency management and is often unexpected.

## Mechanism map: MDMA-like vs LSD-like (added 2026-11-09)
The family splits on **mechanism**, and the split is clean apart from one compound.

- **Entactogen (MDMA-like):** monoamine **releaser** via the transporter; **little 5-HT2A activation**. A 2026 study confirmed MDMA, methylone and MBDB engage 5-HT2A poorly despite strong serotonin release, concluding that extracellular serotonin alone does not drive 5-HT2A-mediated behaviour. ([Nature Neuropsychopharmacology 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-026-03713-1))
- **Classic psychedelic (LSD-like):** direct **5-HT2A agonism**, weak transporter activity.

| Closest to | Compounds | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **MDMA-ish (mixed)** | **2C-B — the only one** | Acts at both 5-HT2A **and** SERT |
| **LSD-ish** | 2C-C, 2C-E, 2C-I, 2C-P, 2C-T series, [[2c-series]] incl. 2C-B-FLY | Predominantly 5-HT2A partial agonists; weak transporter interaction (2C-T data: potent 5-HT2A **and 5-HT2B** partial agonists) |
| **LSD-ish, far more toxic** | [[nbome-nboh]] | Potent 5-HT2A agonists |
| **LSD-ish, very long duration** | [[dox-series]] | 5-HT2A agonists |

**2C-B, the exception.** A 2026 Nature brain-imaging study comparing 2C-B with psilocybin found its entactogenic and cortical effects may derive partly from **monoamine transporter interaction, which classic psychedelics lack**, and reported ~**10-fold difference in 5-HT2A selectivity** between 2C-B and psilocin. ([Nature 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-026-03447-0)) **⚠️→✅ Updated 2026-11-09: that trial has reported, and it settles the question.** The head-to-head comparison of 2C-B with MDMA and psilocybin ([NCT05523401](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05523401), University Hospital Basel) **completed 5 Nov 2024** and was **published** as Arikci et al., *Neuropsychopharmacology* 2026;51(8):1511–1518, doi [10.1038/s41386-026-02428-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41386-026-02428-9) ([PMC13291353](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13291353/)). The repo previously described it as merely registered and its pharmacology "inconclusive" — that is superseded.

Design: **double-blind, placebo-controlled, random-order crossover, n=24** healthy participants (12 women, 12 men), six sessions — placebo, three ascending 2C-B dose levels, an MDMA arm and a psilocybin arm — with ≥10-day washouts and 9 h of measurement. Findings:

- **2C-B is genuinely mixed, dose-dependently so.** At the **high dose** it produced overall drug intensity comparable to the **MDMA** arm but lower than the **psilocybin** arm, increased **emotional empathy like MDMA**, and induced psychedelic-type alterations of consciousness approaching psilocybin — while showing **weaker perceptual effects** (visual/auditory alteration, synaesthesia, ego dissolution) than psilocybin and **stronger** than MDMA. The low dose produced only mild effects and almost no alteration of consciousness.
- **Psilocybin, not 2C-B, was the distressing arm.** **Only psilocybin** significantly increased "bad drug effects" and "anxiety" versus placebo.
- **MDMA was the most cardiostimulant**, then psilocybin, then 2C-B.
- **Duration:** high-dose 2C-B **4.9 h**, MDMA **4.8 h**, psilocybin **6.1 h**. 2C-B plasma elimination half-life **~1.3 h**, dose-proportional PK.
- **Mechanistic divergence confirmed:** only MDMA raised plasma **oxytocin** and neurophysin I — so 2C-B's empathogen-like effect is **not** oxytocin-mediated, unlike MDMA's.
- **Blinding note relevant to the trial-blinding gap:** high-dose 2C-B was correctly identified by only **38%** of participants and was mistaken for psilocybin by another 38%; MDMA and placebo were identified by **79%**.

Two participants withdrew after inclusion, one after a difficult psilocybin session. (Dose levels are deliberately not reproduced here.)

**⚠️ Mechanism similarity is not safety similarity.** The compounds most LSD-like in mechanism (NBOMes) carry this family's worst fatality record. Conversely, transporter-driven serotonin release is what generates **serotonin syndrome** risk — which is why the recorded 2C/NBOMe toxidrome can look serotonergic while LSD's does not. Effect-profile proximity says nothing about margin of safety, duration, or vascular effects.

**Complication to watch:** a 2026 study reports the **α2-adrenergic receptor modulates** 5-HT2A-mediated effects of both MDMA and psilocybin, which muddies the clean two-category model. ([Nature 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-026-03713-1))

Note: 5-HT2B agonism (reported for the 2C-T series) is the receptor implicated in **valvular heart disease** with chronic exposure to other 5-HT2B agonists. No chronic-exposure data exist for any of these compounds.
