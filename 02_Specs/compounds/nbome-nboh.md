# NBOMes and NBOHs (the 25x-NB family)

**Category:** [[psychedelics]]
**Status:** 25B-NBOMe, 25C-NBOMe, 25I-NBOMe and DOC have been under **international control since 2015**. The **NBOH** variants (e.g. 25B-NBOH, 25I-NBOH) are controlled less consistently and are the active substitution route.

## The core hazard: sold as LSD
NBOMes and NBOHs are **regularly sold as LSD on blotter paper**. DEA notes suppliers may "purport or mistake" these for LSD or other Schedule I hallucinogens. In one case series of **42 cases, 9 were fatal** (6 apparent direct NBOMe toxicity, 1 suicide, 2 trauma deaths following agitated delirium) — and **at least 7 of those people believed they had taken LSD**.

This is the whole risk model: the compounds are active at far lower amounts than LSD, so a blotter that looks identical can carry a dangerous quantity. LSD has no comparable record of direct fatal toxicity; these do.

## Recorded toxidrome
- **Tachycardia ~85%**, **hypertension ~65%** — "autonomic instability"
- Can resemble **serotonin syndrome**, unlike LSD
- Seizures, cardiac and respiratory arrest reported at very small amounts (DEA)
- Agitated delirium, with trauma deaths recorded as a consequence

## Telling them apart (harm reduction)
Two cues reported in the literature, neither reliable enough to depend on:
1. **Numbness of the tongue and mouth, then a metallic or chemical taste** when taken sublingually. LSD is tasteless.
2. **Potency mismatch** — if a small fraction of a blotter produces strong effects, that is a red flag rather than good value.
The dependable answer is **reagent or lab drug checking**, not taste. See [[drug-checking]].

## Detection
**Neither NBOMes nor LSD appear on routine urine drug screens.** A negative hospital screen does not exclude either, which matters when a presentation looks like serotonin syndrome of unknown cause.

## 2026 signal
**NIST RaDAR reported 25B-NBOH as a first-time detection in Aug 2026** (single West Coast sample). First appearances of **NBOH** rather than NBOMe variants are consistent with substitution away from the 2015-controlled NBOMes. Low volume so far.
