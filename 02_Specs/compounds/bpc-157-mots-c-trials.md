# BPC-157 and MOTS-c — first controlled human trials

Both were, until 2026, the clearest cases of **strong animal data with no human testing at all**. That changed in February 2026.


## First controlled human trials — registered Feb 2026 (verified on registry 2026-11-16)
| | BPC-157 | MOTS-c |
|---|---|---|
| Trial | **NCT07437547** | **NCT07505745** |
| Title | BPC 157 for Acute Hamstring Muscle Strain Repair | MOTS-c for Improving Insulin Sensitivity in Adults With Prediabetes and Overweight/Obesity |
| Phase / design | Phase 2, RANDOMIZED, QUADRUPLE-masked | identical |
| n | 120 (estimated) | 120 (estimated) |
| Sponsor | Hudson Biotech | Hudson Biotech |
| Start / primary completion | 2026-02-02 / 2027-02-14 | 2026-02-02 / 2027-02-14 |
| Results posted | No | No |

**Read with care:** same sponsor, same start date, same size, same design — this is **one company's programme, not independent validation**. But they are properly controlled trials, which this category has never previously had. **Feb 2027 is the readout** that converts "promising in animals" into an answer either way.


## Why these two were the strongest animal candidates on the grey-market lists
- **BPC-157** — breadth of rodent healing data is unusual: tendon, muscle, gut, nerve, across many injury models. The strongest animal case among the commonly sold recovery peptides. Still: ⚠️ no completed peer-reviewed controlled human trial as of Oct 2026.
- **MOTS-c** — a peptide encoded in **mitochondrial DNA**, which is novel biology in its own right. Protected mice from diet-induced obesity and improved insulin response. ⚠️ FDA briefings found no human data for administered MOTS-c.

## Base rate caveat
Roughly **90% of drugs that work in animals fail in humans**. See [[nad-precursors]] for the live worked example — spectacular mouse data, commercially enormous, failed multi-site ITP replication. "Promising in animals" is a list of candidates with a poor base rate, not a shortlist of winners.

## Not progressing
- **5-Amino-1MQ** — NNMT inhibitor (a small molecule, not a peptide); obese mice lost fat; ⚠️ no registered human trial located as of 2026.
- **KPV** — reduced gut inflammation in murine colitis models; no human trial.
- **TB-500** — tissue repair, weaker and less specific animal data than BPC-157; no human trial.
