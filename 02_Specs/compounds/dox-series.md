# DOx series (DOC, DOI, DOM, DOB)

**Category:** [[psychedelics]]
**Status:** **DOC under international control since 2015** (alongside the three NBOMes). Other members vary.

## Why the risk rating is high despite low trend signal
Three properties compound each other:
1. **Very long duration** — 12–24+ hours is characteristic, considerably longer than LSD. Emergency presentations therefore last far longer than staff may expect, and people often do not know what they took.
2. **Potent at small amounts** — like the NBOMes, suitable for blotter, which means **sold as LSD** is a recurring pattern.
3. **Vasoconstriction** — a mechanism absent from LSD and psilocybin, with peripheral ischaemia reported in the literature for this group.

## Risk notes
- Prolonged agitation, hypertension and tachycardia
- Seizure risk
- **Not on routine drug screens**
- The long duration means re-dosing out of impatience, in the belief that nothing is happening, is a documented route to serious toxicity

## 2026 position
No new DOx first-detections surfaced in the sources checked for Nov 2026. Trend signal is low, but this group has a history of periodic reappearance on blotter, so absence from recent reports is weak evidence. ⚠️ Specific 2026 DOC/DOI detection figures **could not be located** — recorded in [[gaps]].
