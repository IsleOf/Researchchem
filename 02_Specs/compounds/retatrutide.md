# Retatrutide

**Category:** [[weight-loss]]  
**Legal / approval status:** Investigational (Eli Lilly, Phase 3). Not approved anywhere.

## Summary
GLP-1/GIP/glucagon triple agonist. The most talked-about grey-market peptide of 2026; most API is made in China.

## Key risks
- Purity varies widely between batches
- Endotoxin/sterility contamination
- GI effects, heart-rate rise, pancreatitis/gallbladder signals from trials
- Customs seizures and FDA warning letters
