# 2C series (2C-B, 2C-C, 2C-E, 2C-I, 2C-P and others)

**Category:** [[psychedelics]]
**Status:** 2C-B is under international control. Other members vary by jurisdiction; **UNODC describes the 2C series as the largest of these sub-families**, with reported members still increasing.

## Position in 2026
- **Phenethylamines are a top-three NPS poisoning contributor** in the Netherlands: with cathinones and benzodiazepines they account for **83% of NPS poisonings** over 2012–2025.
- The same analysis reports a **relative increase for 2C-B**, alongside ketamine and LSD. ⚠️ Period and context **unverified** (truncated extract).
- **2C-C recorded as a first-time RaDAR detection in Aug 2026** (several West Coast samples).
- **Prodrugs — correction logged 2026-11-09.** An earlier version of this note implied 2C-B-AN was a 2026 emergence. **It is not:** it was encountered as a designer drug in Europe **by 2016**. The Jan 2026 DEA reference merely mentions it. **No prevalence or seizure data for 2C-B-AN could be located, for 2026 or any year** — treat its market presence as unknown and probably marginal. It is also described as chemically unstable, which plausibly limits it as a product. The general point still stands: prodrugs fall outside control language written for the parent compound, the same structural gambit as the designer-precursor pattern in [[precursor-space]].

## The FLY compounds — the genuine 2025–26 emergence
- **2C-B-FLY** — **first identified by CFSRE's laboratory in Feb 2025**; unscheduled in the US at that time. 5-HT2A partial agonist, like 2C-B. ([CFSRE monograph](https://www.cfsre.org/nps-discovery/monographs/2c-b-fly))
- **Why this matters more than the prodrug:** the FLY family's close relative **Bromo-DragonFLY** has a documented record of **extremely long duration and severe vasoconstriction**, including limb ischaemia and amputation. If 2C-B-FLY resembles its relatives, duration and vascular injury are the hazards rather than acute overdose. ⚠️ 2C-B-FLY's own duration and vascular profile in humans is **not characterised** — this is family-based inference.
- **2C-YN** also appears in the literature.

## Scale of the family
UNODC has **>180 phenethylamines** reported to it, about **80 classed as classical hallucinogens**, most sharing the 2,5-dimethoxy ring pattern across the 2C, 2D and NBOMe families. Most members have never appeared in a drug supply, so a single new detection is weak evidence of a trend.

## Risk notes
- **Members differ sharply in potency and duration** — the shared "2C-" prefix implies a similarity that does not hold. 2C-P in particular is far more potent and much longer-acting than 2C-B, and has been implicated in poisonings where users expected 2C-B-like effects.
- **Vasoconstrictive and serotonergic effects** in parts of the series.
- **Nausea and vomiting** are common and in some members near-universal.
- Mis-selling within the series — one 2C compound sold as another — carries the same structural risk as the LSD/NBOMe problem, in milder form.
- **Not on routine drug screens.**
