# Bromazolam

**Category:** [[depressants]]  
**Legal / approval status:** Controlled in UK, many EU states, and **US Schedule I by temporary order effective 16 Mar 2026 to 16 Mar 2028** ([FR 2026-05064](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/03/16/2026-05064/schedules-of-controlled-substances-temporary-placement-of-bromazolam-in-schedule-i), Docket DEA-1420; corrected for an invalid chemical name by [FR 2026-12653](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/24/2026-12653/schedules-of-controlled-substances-placement-of-bromazolam-in-schedule-i-correction)). The FR order cites **259 US overdose cases Apr 2021–Feb 2026** in all 50 states, likely under-reported. **Verified 2026-11-09** — note DEA's press release is dated 18 Mar 2026, which is not the effective date.

## Summary
Dominant designer benzodiazepine in street samples 2024–26, often pressed as fake Xanax or mixed into opioids.

## Key risks
- Respiratory depression with opioids/alcohol
- Blackouts, amnesia
- Seizures on withdrawal
- Naloxone does not reverse benzos
