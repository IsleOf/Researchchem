# NAD+ precursors (NAD+ IV/injection, NMN, NR) and resveratrol

**Status:** Sold as supplements and IV clinic services. **NMN**: FDA excluded it from the dietary-supplement definition in 2022 under the drug-preclusion clause, then **reversed that in late 2025** after a lawsuit and petitions, accepting evidence of marketing as a supplement from 2017. NR and resveratrol are ordinary supplements. IV NAD+ is sold as a clinic service, not an approved therapy.

## The question people actually ask
"Are NAD+ shots better than oral NMN (± resveratrol)?" **No head-to-head RCT exists.** More importantly, the comparison presumes an effect to compare: both reliably raise a **blood marker**, and neither has demonstrated a **clinical outcome**.

## Mechanism caveat on the IV route
NAD+ is a large charged molecule that does not cross cell membranes intact. Injected NAD+ is degraded extracellularly to smaller precursors, which are what cells take up. "Direct delivery" is therefore partly illusory — the same destination by a costlier route. The marketed **"100% bioavailability"** figure is a claim about reaching the bloodstream, not about reaching cells.

## Human evidence
- **Oral NMN, 24-week RCT, adults 65+, 1,250 mg/day:** NAD+ and related metabolites rose significantly, but **no significant improvement in the primary outcome (hearing) or in physical performance**. Authors' own conclusion: evidence for broader physiological benefit remains limited.
- **2024 meta-analysis**, 12 RCTs, n=513: NAD levels rise vs placebo, **high heterogeneity (I²=80%)**.
- **NR has the most human data** of the three — ~60% increase in PBMC NAD+ at 1,000 mg/day over 6 weeks.
- **IV NAD+:** human efficacy data described in the literature as limited. A pilot compared IV placebo / IV NAD+ / IV NR / oral NR; a retrospective tolerability study exists. ⚠️ No peer-reviewed head-to-head vs oral NMN or NR located.
- **Mechanism finding:** NMN and NR both roughly double circulating NAD+ at 14 days while nicotinamide does not — and **gut bacteria appear to convert NMN and NR to nicotinic acid**, which may do much of the work. If so the oral route is part of the mechanism, not a bottleneck being bypassed.

## Resveratrol — the weakest link in the common stack
- **Oral bioavailability <1%** (rapid metabolism and urinary excretion).
- **2025 systematic review + dose-response meta-analysis, 11 RCTs: no significant effect on human SIRT1.** Gene expression SMD 0.05 (95% CI −0.24 to 0.344, P=.73); same for protein expression. ⚠️ Measured SIRT1 **levels**, not enzyme **activity** — so not fully dispositive. Scattered subgroup signals exist in small trials of mostly overweight populations.
- **The original activation result looks assay-dependent:** using native substrates (full-length p53) instead of the fluorophore-tagged short peptide, resveratrol **did not activate SIRT1**.
- **Consequence for the stack:** the NMN+resveratrol rationale is a chain — NAD+ fuels sirtuins, resveratrol activates them. The second link is the contested one. Without it, the combination is NMN plus a poorly absorbed polyphenol, not a synergy.

## Animal evidence — better than the human data, and still negative where it counts
The **NIA Interventions Testing Program (ITP)** exists to catch single-lab false positives: three independent sites, genetically heterogeneous mice, both sexes, properly powered.

| Compound | ITP / NIA result |
|---|---|
| Resveratrol (300 & 1200 ppm, two cohorts) | **No increase** in median or maximum lifespan |
| Nicotinamide riboside (1000 ppm, from 8 mo) | **No increase** in lifespan |
| Nicotinamide (NIA intramural) | Health improvements, **no change in longevity** |

**Every NAD-pathway compound that reached the replication standard failed it.**

- **Resveratrol's famous 2006 result was in obese mice on a high-fat diet** — it did **not** extend lifespan in normally fed mice. Conditional on an unhealthy baseline even before it failed replication.
- **NMN** has a surviving positive, but: healthspan benefits at 12 months (not lifespan); the lifespan result (+8.5% median, **females only**) is **single-lab, sex-specific, preprint/abstract**; and a **large NIA study of both sexes saw no effect**. That is precisely the profile the ITP was built to test.
- **Proposed explanation for NR's failure:** by the time it reached blood it had already been metabolised to plain nicotinamide — consistent with the human gut-conversion finding above.

## Bottom line
Animal work supports healthspan-type benefits in ageing or metabolically unhealthy mice; it mostly **fails to show lifespan extension under rigorous replication**. Human work reliably moves a marker with no demonstrated outcome. Nothing in either body of evidence distinguishes injection from oral. Best-evidenced of the three is **oral NR**, and "best-evidenced" here still means moving a marker.

## Open questions
- ⚠️ Peer-reviewed status of the female-specific NMN lifespan result
- ⚠️ Whether SIRT1 *activity* (not level) responds to resveratrol in humans
- ⚠️ Any controlled comparison of IV NAD+ against an oral precursor
