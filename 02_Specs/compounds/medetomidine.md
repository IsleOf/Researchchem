# Medetomidine

**Category:** [[depressants]]  
**Legal / approval status:** Veterinary sedative; not for human use.

## Summary
Alpha-2 agonist replacing xylazine as fentanyl adulterant (81% of Toronto fentanyl samples late 2025; 92% xylazine/medetomidine in Philadelphia).

## Key risks
- Very slow heart rate, low blood pressure
- Severe withdrawal syndrome
- Not reversed by naloxone
