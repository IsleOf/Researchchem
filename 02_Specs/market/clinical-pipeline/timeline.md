## Earlier anchors carried forward (pre-2025, for context)
- **2024-12** — FDA approves **Zepbound (tirzepatide) for moderate-to-severe obstructive sleep apnoea** in adults with obesity (SURMOUNT-OSA; 469 adults; 52 weeks). First drug ever approved for OSA.
- **2024-08** — FDA issues **Complete Response Letter** for MDMA-assisted therapy in PTSD; additional phase 3 requested.
- **2024-04** — EMA PRAC concludes available evidence **does not support** a causal association between GLP-1 receptor agonists and suicidal/self-injurious thoughts and actions.
- **2024-03** — FDA grants **Breakthrough Therapy designation** to MM120 for generalized anxiety disorder.
- **2024** — **FLOW** published (semaglutide, CKD in type 2 diabetes), NEJM doi 10.1056/NEJMoa2403347.
- **2023** — **SELECT** published (semaglutide, MACE in obesity without diabetes), NEJM doi 10.1056/NEJMoa2307563. BPC-157 placed in **Category 2** of FDA's 503A bulk substances list.
