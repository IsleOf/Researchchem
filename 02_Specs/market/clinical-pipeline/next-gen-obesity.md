# Next-generation obesity pharmacotherapy (L1)

Part of [[clinical-pipeline/index]]. Related: [[clinical-pipeline/glp1-beyond-weight]], [[clinical-pipeline/muscle-preservation]], [[clinical-pipeline/risks]].

**Convention in this note:** per repo rules, efficacy is reported by *trial arm label* ("highest-dose arm", "mid-dose arm") and duration. No mg values, no titration schedules. Sponsor press releases are flagged `[sponsor]`; peer-reviewed publications and regulator documents are not.

---

## 1. Orforglipron — oral small-molecule GLP-1 receptor agonist (Lilly)

The single biggest structural change in the field 2025–26: a non-peptide, orally bioavailable GLP-1 agonist with no food or water restriction, which removes the cold-chain/injection constraint that defined the market.

- **ATTAIN-1** (obesity without diabetes), n=3127, 72 weeks, placebo-controlled. Highest-dose arm mean body-weight change **−11.2% vs −2.1%** placebo (treatment-regimen estimand). Adverse events led to discontinuation in **5.3–10.3%** across orforglipron arms vs **2.7%** placebo. Mostly GI AEs; five cases of mild pancreatitis and a modest heart-rate increase reported. N Engl J Med 2025, doi 10.1056/NEJMoa2511774, PMID 40960239, NCT05869903.
- A widely quoted **~12.4%** figure for the same arm is the *efficacy* estimand / topline release, not the treatment-regimen number above. Both circulate; they are not different trials.
- **ATTAIN-2** (obesity/overweight + type 2 diabetes), phase 3, 72 weeks. Placebo-subtracted weight reduction up to **−7.1%** (95% CI 6.1–8.2); maximum HbA1c reduction **−1.1%**. AE discontinuations **6.1–9.9%** vs **4.1%** placebo. Lancet 2025;406, PIIS0140-6736(25)02165-8, NCT05872620.
- **ATTAIN-J** (Japanese participants with obesity disease), phase 3 — Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol 2026, PIIS2213-8587(26)00232-9.
- **ACHIEVE-3** — open-label non-inferiority phase 3 vs oral semaglutide in type 2 diabetes. Lancet 2026, PIIS0140-6736(26)00202-3.
- **ACHIEVE-4** — longest phase 3 to date; sponsor reports reaffirmed cardiovascular and overall safety profile `[sponsor]`.
- **Regulatory:** FDA **approved 1 April 2026** as **Foundayo** for chronic weight management in adults with obesity, or overweight plus ≥1 weight-related comorbidity, alongside reduced-calorie diet and increased activity. Granted under the Commissioner's **National Priority Voucher (CNPV)** pilot — the fifth CNPV approval and, per FDA, the fastest new-molecular-entity approval since 2002. US launch April 2026. A **type 2 diabetes** indication was not included in the April approval and was filed separately `[sponsor]`.
- FDA: "FDA Approves First New Molecular Entity Under National Priority Voucher Program" https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-new-molecular-entity-under-national-priority-voucher-program

## 2. Retatrutide — GLP-1 / GIP / glucagon triple agonist (Lilly)

The efficacy ceiling-setter. Still **investigational everywhere** as of 2026-11.

- **TRIUMPH-1** (obesity without diabetes) — **✅ verified 2026-11-09**. **n=2335** (actual enrolment per registry; the trial report states 2339 randomised), **80 weeks**, **four arms, 1:1:1:1** (low / mid / high dose, placebo), 131 sites in 11 countries, mean baseline BMI 40.0. Mean body-weight change **−17.6%, −23.7%, −25.0%** vs **−3.9%** placebo. *N Engl J Med*, doi [10.1056/NEJMoa2604169](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2604169), online 29 Sep 2026, [NCT05929066](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05929066) (registry status **completed** 30 Apr 2026; **no results posted on the registry**).
  - **Estimand label corrected.** Those headline figures are the **treatment-regimen** estimand. The **efficacy** estimand for the same arms is **−19.0%, −25.9%, −28.3%** vs **−2.2%**. Quoting one set against the other is the standard error in this literature — see [[clinical-pipeline/open-questions]] item 9. Per-arm estimand values confirmed in a peer-reviewed critical review (*Pharmaceutics* 2026;18(10):1277, doi [10.3390/pharmaceutics18101277](https://doi.org/10.3390/pharmaceutics18101277), [PMC13708278](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13708278/)).
  - **AE discontinuations: 4.1% / 6.9% / 11.3% vs 4.9% placebo.** Note the low-dose arm discontinued *less* than placebo; the review warns explicitly that this "is not evidence of superior safety."
  - Per-arm GI events (low/mid/high vs placebo): nausea **28.6 / 38.4 / 42.4 vs 14.8%**; vomiting **10.6 / 22.8 / 25.3 vs 4.8%**; diarrhoea **25.2 / 34.1 / 32.0 vs 13.5%**; constipation **23.8 / 25.9 / 26.1 vs 10.9%**; **dysaesthesia 5.1 / 12.3 / 12.5 vs 0.9%**.
- **Apnoea–hypopnoea index (OSA basket)**: **−22.9 (low), −34.3 (mid), −31.7 (high)** events/hour at 80 weeks vs **−9.9** placebo (hybrid-treatment estimand). ⚠️ **Corrected**: these figures are reported in the **TRIUMPH-1 primary paper's own abstract**, not only in a separate substudy publication, and the repo previously **omitted the low-dose value (−22.9)**. A separate OSA substudy report (N Engl J Med, online 21 Oct 2026, PMID 42904878) was **not retrievable** for this check — do not treat the two as independent results until that is confirmed.
- **TRIUMPH-4** (obesity + knee osteoarthritis), **n=445** (actual, registry), **68 weeks**, three arms (mid / high dose, placebo), co-primary weight + WOMAC pain. Sponsor topline Dec 2025 — efficacy estimand **−26.4% / −28.7%** vs **−2.1%** placebo; treatment-regimen **−20.0% / −23.7%** vs **−4.6%**. WOMAC pain (0–10 scale) **−4.5 / −4.4 points vs −2.4 placebo**. ⚠️ The previously held "4.5 points (**75.8%**)" dropped the placebo comparator and the 75.8% figure could not be sourced — **placebo-adjusted the pain benefit is roughly 2 points, not 4.5**. **AE discontinuations 12.2% / 18.2% vs 4.0% placebo** — **confirmed**, still the highest in the class; the sponsor attributes the excess partly to participants with *lower* baseline BMI, including perceived excessive weight loss. Dysaesthesia reached **20.9%** in the high-dose arm. [NCT05931367](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05931367) (completed 14 Nov 2025; **no registry results**). The BMI ≥35 subgroup figures (8.8% / 12.1% vs 4.8%) remain `[sponsor]`-only and **unverified**.
- **TRIUMPH-3** (severe obesity + established cardiovascular disease), n=1949, 1:1:2 randomisation, sponsor July 2026: efficacy-estimand **−21.6% / −22.6%** vs **−3.2%** placebo; AE discontinuations **9.8% / 13.5% vs 4.8%** — **confirmed** against the same review; MACE occurred less often than anticipated in both arms, i.e. the trial was not powered as a CV outcomes trial `[sponsor]`.
- **TRIUMPH-2** (obesity + type 2 diabetes), n=1152, 80 weeks: efficacy-estimand **−12.7% / −19.1% / −20.8%** vs **−4.0%**; HbA1c **−1.4 / −1.6 / −1.5** points vs **−0.2**; AE discontinuations **3.8% / 11.6% / 7.7% vs 4.9%**. Added 2026-11-09 — illustrates the ~one-third efficacy loss in T2D noted in [[clinical-pipeline/open-questions]].
- **Provenance caveat on all four.** As of a 11 Sep 2026 literature cutoff, **only TRANSCEND-T2D-1 had a full peer-reviewed report**; TRIUMPH-1 to -4 weight and safety results were **sponsor topline disclosures and congress presentations**. TRIUMPH-1 has since been published (29 Sep 2026); **TRIUMPH-2, -3 and -4 remain topline-only**, and denominators, analysis populations and severity gradings are not fully specified in those disclosures.
- A 104-week extension figure of **~30.3%** in a BMI ≥35 subgroup circulates from secondary coverage — **unverified** against a primary publication; treat as not established.
- Head-to-head vs tirzepatide registered as NCT06662383.
- **Regulatory:** sponsor has signalled a BLA filing timeline of Q1 2027 `[sponsor]`; **unverified** against an FDA docket.

## 3. CagriSema — cagrilintide (amylin analogue) + semaglutide, fixed combination (Novo Nordisk)

- **REDEFINE 1** (overweight/obesity without diabetes), n=3417 randomised (CagriSema 2108 / semaglutide 302 / cagrilintide 302 / placebo 705), 68 weeks. Mean body-weight change **−20.4% vs −3.0%** placebo (difference −17.3 pp, 95% CI −18.1 to −16.6, P<0.001). N Engl J Med, doi 10.1056/NEJMoa2502081.
- The **22.7%** headline is the *trial-product / full-adherence* estimand (vs 2.3% placebo; semaglutide alone 16.1%, cagrilintide alone 11.8%); **40.4%** lost ≥25% of body weight on that estimand `[sponsor]`. Quoting 22.7% as the trial result without the estimand caveat is the single most common misreading of this programme.
- Secondary analysis: systolic BP reduced ~**10.9 mmHg** at 68 weeks (PMC12822771). Target-attainment/cardiometabolic post hoc: PMC13538786.
- **REDEFINE 2** (type 2 diabetes), 68 weeks: **−13.7% vs −3.1%** placebo — markedly less than the non-diabetes population, the usual pattern across this class `[sponsor]`.
- **REDEFINE 4** — head-to-head vs tirzepatide, 84 weeks: **missed its primary endpoint of non-inferiority** on weight loss. This is the most important negative result of 2026 in obesity and reframed CagriSema from "best-in-class" to "another option" `[sponsor]`.
- **Regulatory:** weight-management application submitted to FDA **December 2025** on REDEFINE 1 + 2; sponsor restated a **Q4 2026** decision window; **no public PDUFA date** and **not approved** as of 2026-11. Type 2 diabetes is being pursued on a separate pathway `[sponsor]`.

## 4. Amycretin / zenagamtide — unimolecular GLP-1 + amylin co-agonist (Novo Nordisk)

- Phase 2 in **type 2 diabetes**, n=448, **36 weeks**: subcutaneous arms weight loss up to **−14.5%** (restated as up to −14.6% at the highest dose investigated, ADA 2026); oral arms HbA1c reduction up to **−1.5%** and weight loss up to **−10.1%**. Tolerability described as consistent with incretin/amylin agents `[sponsor]`.
- Now referred to as **zenagamtide**; sponsor says it advances into an extensive multi-indication phase 3 programme. Specific phase 3 trial names circulating in secondary sources (e.g. AMAZE, AMBITION, AMBIENCE) are **unverified** here.
- Figures of ~22–25% weight loss attributed to amycretin come from small early-phase cohorts or secondary aggregators and **do not match sponsor-reported phase 2 numbers**. Treat as unverified.
- Note the population: these are **diabetes** data, 36 weeks, not an obesity phase 3 — not comparable to the 68–80-week obesity trials above.

## 5. MariTide / maridebart cafraglutide — GLP-1 agonist + GIP receptor **antagonist**, once-monthly (Amgen)

Mechanistically the odd one out: it *blocks* GIP rather than agonising it, and dosing is monthly rather than weekly.

- **Phase 2**, n=592, **52 weeks**, 11 groups in two cohorts. Obesity cohort: weight loss ~**12% to 16%** vs ~**2.5%** placebo. Obesity + type 2 diabetes cohort: ~**8% to 12%** vs ~**1.7%** placebo; HbA1c **−1.2 to −1.6 pp** vs +0.1 pp. GI AEs common, reduced by lower starting dose and escalation. **Gallbladder events 1–6%** in MariTide obesity-cohort arms vs **0%** placebo, highest in the top-dose arm. N Engl J Med 2025;393:843–857, doi 10.1056/NEJMoa2504214, PMID 40549887.
- A subsequent NEJM correspondence (doi 10.1056/NEJMc2514331) notes participants had **not reached a weight plateau by 52 weeks**.
- **Phase 3 MARITIME programme** running; MARITIME-1 and MARITIME-2 primary completion listed as **January 2027**, so **no phase 3 efficacy results exist** as of 2026-11. Extensions NCT07684235, NCT07684144; switch study NCT07575399; obesity + HFpEF NCT07037459; Japan NCT06987695; elevated liver fat NCT07441252. A cardiovascular outcomes trial (MARITIME-CV, NCT07037433) is registered; its ~12,800 enrolment figure is from secondary coverage and **unverified**.
- No marketing application publicly announced.

## 6. Petrelintide — long-acting amylin analogue monotherapy (Zealand Pharma / Roche)

The tolerability play rather than the efficacy play.

- **ZUPREME-1**, n=493, adults with overweight or obesity, **42 weeks**. Highest-dose arm **−10.7%** mean weight reduction vs **−1.7%** placebo. **Vomiting was less frequent than placebo** (none in the most effective arm); nausea **19.6%** vs **6.2%** placebo; **trial dropouts lower on drug (8.4%) than placebo (13.6%)** `[sponsor]`. Published in Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology (2026) — exact DOI **not confirmed here**.
- **ZUPREME-2** (obesity + type 2 diabetes), phase 2b, **28 weeks**: up to **−9.2%** weight loss, primary endpoint met `[sponsor]`.
- **Phase 3 ZUPREME programme** initiated 2026 `[sponsor]`.
- Analyst split is the honest summary: the weight-loss magnitude is modest against incretins, and the whole case rests on a GI profile that has only phase 2 support. Cardiovascular outcomes are absent.

## 7. Survodutide — GLP-1 / glucagon dual agonist (Boehringer Ingelheim / Zealand)

- **SYNCHRONIZE-1** (overweight/obesity without diabetes), **76 weeks**: highest-dose arm up to **−16.6%** vs **−3.2%** placebo; co-primary endpoints met on both efficacy and treatment-regimen estimands. GI AEs common, mostly mild–moderate and transient; discontinuations concentrated during dose escalation. Presented ADA 2026; sponsor states NEJM publication — citation **not confirmed here** `[sponsor]`.
- **SYNCHRONIZE-2** (obesity + type 2 diabetes), **76 weeks**, Oct 2026: up to **−13.1%** vs **−3.1%** placebo; HbA1c reduction up to **−1.21 pp** `[sponsor]`. NCT06066528.
- **SYNCHRONIZE-MASLD** — phase 3 in obesity with MASLD; co-primary endpoints met on both estimands; up to **84.2%** of efficacy-estimand participants achieved ≥30% relative liver-fat reduction. Published in Nature Medicine; PMC13472939. Note this is **MASLD (liver fat), not MASH histology** — see [[clinical-pipeline/glp1-beyond-weight]].
- SYNCHRONIZE-START registered as NCT07855900. No regulatory filing for obesity confirmed as of 2026-11.

## 8. Mazdutide — GLP-1 / glucagon dual agonist (Innovent, China; Lilly ex-China)

The first approval in a genuinely new incretin class, and it happened in China first.

- **GLORY-1**, n=610 Chinese adults with overweight or obesity, **48 weeks**, two dose arms + placebo. Mean body-weight change approximately **−11.0%** (low-dose arm) and **−14.0%** (high-dose arm) vs **+0.3%** placebo; placebo-adjusted difference for the high-dose arm **−14.31%** (treatment policy) / **−14.37%** (efficacy). **AE discontinuations 1.5% / 0.5% vs 1.0% placebo** — strikingly low versus the Western trials above, which may reflect population, dose ceiling and trial conduct rather than a true tolerability advantage. N Engl J Med, doi 10.1056/NEJMoa2411528, NCT05607680.
- **Regulatory:** China NMPA approved **June 2025** for chronic weight management (brand Xinermei), and **September 2025** for glycaemic control in type 2 diabetes. First dual GCG/GLP-1 receptor agonist approved anywhere. Reviewed as "Mazdutide: First Approval", PMID 41028652. **Not approved outside China**; rights outside China held by Lilly.
- A US-based multicentre phase 2 has been published: PMID 42628555, NCT06124807.

## Market-signal observation (no sourcing information recorded)

Searching the open web for every agent in this note returns, alongside the journals and regulator pages, consumer-facing "peptide"/"research chemical" catalogue and affiliate pages. In the course of this research such pages appeared for **retatrutide, tirzepatide, semaglutide, cagrilintide, mazdutide, survodutide, orforglipron, maridebart cafraglutide, petrelintide, apitegromab and elamipretide/SS-31**. Recorded purely as a market signal: grey-market catalogues track the registrational pipeline closely and often list agents that have **no completed phase 3** (petrelintide, MariTide) or **no approval anywhere** (retatrutide, survodutide). The gap between a trial arm and an unregulated vial — identity, purity, sterility, actual content — is not addressed by any of the evidence above. No vendor, marketplace or sourcing details are recorded in this knowledge base.
