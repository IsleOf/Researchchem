# Cathinone subclasses, named detections and supply geography

## The three families behave differently
| Family | Members seen 2025–26 | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Pyrrolidinophenones ("pyrros")** | α-PVP, α-PHP, α-PiHP, α-PBP, 4F-α-PVP, MDPV, MDPHP, MDPiHP; ring-methyl isomers 2-/3-/4-Me-α-PiHP | Severity concentrates here. DAT-selective → cardiac events, psychosis |
| **"-ylones" (β-keto methylenedioxy)** | eutylone, dipentylone, N-isopropylbutylone, N-propylbutylone | Where US substitution churn concentrates |
| **"-drones" (β-keto amphetamines)** | 4-MMC, 3-MMC, 3-CMC, 4-CMC, 2-MMC | European bulk volume |

## Named detections
- US case series 2026: α-PVP in 45 cases, MDPV in 17; most frequent were α-PBP, MDPHP, 4F-α-PVP; N-propylbutylone in 7 ([PMID 41830777](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41830777/))
- UK ACMD detection counts: eutylone 293, dipentylone 138, α-PiHP 125, α-PVP 91 (timeframe unclear in source — treat as indicative)
- **Isomer churn, measured:** Sweden — 4-Me-α-PiHP emerged 2020, 2-Me-α-PiHP 2022, 3-Me-α-PiHP 2023; 113 seizures total. Same scaffold, methyl position moved, legal status reset each time.
- **US replacement event:** N,N-dimethylpentylone controlled Aug 2025 → N-isopropylbutylone took its place; eutylone receding.
- **EU control 12 Jan 2026:** 2-MMC, 4-BMC, NEP — 2-MMC was the main 3-MMC substitute, so this control is itself the next substitution trigger.
- **α-PVP** flagged by EUDA as growing concern: high potency, agitation, paranoia, aggression, psychosis.
- US NFLIS 2010–2024 trend analysis: substantial temporal, regional and subclass variation ([PMID 42581582](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42581582/))

## Supply geography by volume (published monitoring data)
**EU production sites, 2023** (clearest country split published): 53 cathinone sites — **Poland 40, Netherlands 8**, remainder Germany, Belgium, Austria, Sweden. By product: 24× 4-CMC, 20× 4-MMC, 7× 3-CMC, 1× α-PVP.
**2024:** 65 illicit labs dismantled; EUDA states production "remains significant, particularly in Poland" — no 2024 country split published.
**Precursor seizures 2023:** 2,153 kg total — **Netherlands 1,416 kg, Poland 735 kg** (up from 558 kg total in 2022).
**Origins where known:** China and India. Destinations: Poland, Netherlands, Spain, UK. Finished bulk cathinone: a small number of **bulk imports from India via the Netherlands** accounted for most reported material.
**Product-to-place:** 3-CMC production focused on Poland, smaller roles Netherlands and Slovakia. 4-CMC concentrated in Poland.

## Policy moves that will shift the above
- **Netherlands generic (blanket) cathinone control, July 2025** — removes the main entry point's legal headroom.
- **EU controlled nine drug precursors in 2026** after its first in-house precursor assessments (individual assessments published by EUDA).
