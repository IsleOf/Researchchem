# Semi-synthetic cannabinoid churn (2025–26)

**40 semi-synthetic cannabinoids monitored by EUDA at end-2025; 27 first identified during 2025.** The fastest-growing NPS group by count.

## Sequence
1. **HHC** — first identified, placed under **international control Dec 2025**.
2. **HHC-O** (acetate) and **HHC-P** sold as replacements. EUDA notes HHC-P "seems to be fully synthetic" rather than CBD-derived — the "semi-synthetic" category is breaking down into fully synthetic chemistry.
3. **H4CBD** (saturated CBD) — in Europe since Dec 2022, the most frequently detected derivative in German seizures, still unscheduled in many countries, **no human studies**.
4. **Δ8- / Δ9-THCP** (heptyl analogues) — appeared Mar 2023.
5. **10-OH-HHC**.

## Format shift
E-liquids and vapes were the growth product form; many EU member states reported seizing cannabinoid e-liquids. 27 new cannabinoids notified in 2025, 16 of them semi-synthetic. **3.2 tonnes + 200 litres** of HHC seized (most of the SSC seizure volume).

## Regulation
- Netherlands: HHC, HHC-P, THCP to hard-drugs list **Jan 2026**; derivatives incl. 10-OH-HHC and H4CBD covered since Dec 2025.
- No harmonised EU-wide SSC ban expected in 2026.

## Risk
EUDA: pharmacological similarity to Δ9-THC raises concern for **psychotic episodes, abuse and dependence**. Product labels frequently do not match contents (US testing). Potency per unit is unknown to the user for most of these.
