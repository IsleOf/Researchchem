# Market-structure timeline, 2024–2026

Up: [[market-structure/index]]

Newest first. Dates marked **(unverified)** come from single or secondary sources and need a primary check.

## 2026

- **2026-10-16** — Partnership for Safe Medicines files a **citizen petition asking FDA not to permit compounding** of the six peptides PCAC recommended, citing the 2026 endotoxin recalls. → [[market-structure/peptide-compounding-policy]]
- **2026-10-01** — FDA posts **Greenwich Rx** nationwide recall of compounded glutathione for elevated endotoxin (product distributed 26 Jun – 21 Sep 2026).
- **2026-09-24** — **Apothecary Pharma** (NC) nationwide recall of compounded **tirzepatide** injection; sterility could not be assured.
- **2026-09-01** — FDA posts **five warning letters dated 2026-08-24** to online research-peptide sellers; intended use judged from the whole website presentation, "research use only" disclaimer rejected. Products named across letters: retatrutide, semaglutide, tirzepatide, tesamorelin, SS-31, PT-141. **(secondary-sourced)**
- **2026-08-27** — Federal Register: further temporary Schedule I placement (benzimidazole-type), referencing an HHS letter of 2026-04-01.
- **2026-08-27** — Fifth Circuit reported to affirm FDA's tirzepatide shortage determination. **(unverified, single source)**
- **2026-08-19** — **Optimal Balance Pharmacy** (Houston) recalls compounded glutathione to patient level for elevated endotoxin; reported adverse events include fever, chills, severe headache, nausea, vomiting, allergy-like symptoms.
- **2026-08 (early)** — **Victory Medical Center Pharmacy** (Austin) recalls three glutathione lots for elevated endotoxin. FDA notes both Texas pharmacies sourced glutathione **labelled dietary-supplement grade from the same manufacturer**.
- **2026-08** — WEDINOS publishes its **April 2025 – March 2026 annual report**: 8,209 samples; **36.6% of purchase-intent community samples not the expected substance**; 43% of cannabinoid-vape samples contained synthetic cannabinoids; **orphine analogues identified for the first time**. → [[market-structure/drug-checking-data]]
- **2026-08** — UNODC EWA passes **1,500+ substances** reported worldwide.
- **2026-07-23/24** — **PCAC meeting** on seven peptides (BPC-157, KPV, TB-500, MOTS-c, emideltide/DSIP, Semax, Epitalon); reported outcome **six recommended, DSIP rejected**. Advisory only; none placed on the 503A list. **(vote detail secondary-sourced)**
- **2026-06-09** — **European Drug Report 2026**: 50 NPS first detected in Europe in 2025 (total 1,050); seven new synthetic opioids; growing EWS reports of counterfeit medicines containing nitazenes; **orphine rise attributed to China's July 2025 nitazene ban**; nitazenes degrade post-mortem so deaths likely undercounted; 2024 synthetic-opioid seizures **35.5 kg / 1,063 seizures** vs 22 kg in 2023.
- **2026-06** — Clinical press reports a surge of medication errors and adverse events associated with rapid telehealth expansion in compounded GLP-1 prescribing.
- **2026-05-12** — DEA public safety advisory: **22 unique nitazenes since 2020, 21 Schedule I**; nitazenes frequently mixed into counterfeit pills or fentanyl powder without the user's knowledge.
- **2026-04-30** — FDA proposes **permanently excluding semaglutide, tirzepatide and liraglutide from the 503B bulks list**.
- **2026-04-24** — Preprint **"Evaluation of Research Grade Peptides Marketed Directly to Consumers…"** posted (DOI 10.20944/preprints202604.1748.v1): **41.6% fail** under a liberal compounding-standards model, **71.1%** under a stricter one; **~15% of endotoxin-tested samples endotoxin-positive**; **purity does not predict endotoxin (R²<0.01, P=0.75)**. Not peer-reviewed; authorship and exact N **unverified**.
- **2026-04 (mid)** — FDA announces removal of **12 peptides from Category 2** of the interim 503A bulks list **because nominators withdrew their nominations** — procedural, not a safety clearance and not an authorisation to compound. **GHK-Cu moved the other way**, removed from Category 1, PCAC review expected before end Feb 2027. (Count given as 11 or 12; date as 15 or 22 April.)
- **2026-04-06** — Utah physician indicted in a case involving 200+ patients supplied unapproved imported peptides.
- **2026-03-31 / 2026-04-07** — Batch of CDER warning letters (reported as seven) to sellers of GLP-1 analogues. **(secondary-sourced)**
- **2026-03-25** — CBP reports interdicting **~5,000 peptide shipments in >300 China-origin master cartons** at the Port of Cincinnati (retatrutide, semaglutide, tirzepatide). Window given as Dec 2025 – Mar 2026 **or** Dec 2024 – Mar 2025 — **unresolved**.
- **2026-03-18** — DEA places **bromazolam** in Schedule I by emergency temporary order.
- **2026-03** — EUDA/SCORE wastewater round published: **130 cities, 28 countries**, sampling Jan–May 2025; **ketamine +~41%**, **MDMA −16%** 2024→2025 in cities reporting both years. Panel excludes cathinones, nitazenes and peptides.
- **2026-03** — UN **CND places three NPS under international control**. UNODC EWA register at 1,444 substances.
- **2026-03-03** — FDA/HHS reported to send warning letters to a further ~30 telehealth companies over compounded GLP-1 marketing. **(secondary-sourced)**
- **2026** — HPRA (Ireland) reports a **~30-fold rise in detained GLP-1 products**. **(unverified)**
- **2026** — EU brings three cathinones (**2-MMC, 4-BMC, NEP**) under control as drugs in EU law.
- **2026** — *Lancet Psychiatry* commentary: **routine toxicology screens do not include nitazenes**; test strips detect only **78% of analogues** and false-positive on caffeine.
- **2026-02** — Gardner et al. accepted in *Drug Testing and Analysis* (doi:10.1002/dta.70045): 47 UK bromazolam tablets, **55% mimicked licensed alprazolam/diazepam**, median 0.49 mg, range **0.09–5.4 mg**, within-batch 1.66–2.41 mg.
- **2026-01-12** — Federal Register: **permanent Schedule I** for N-pyrrolidino metonitazene and N-pyrrolidino protonitazene.

## 2025

- **2025-12** — UNODC *Current NPS Threats* (2024 data): **688 unique NPS** detected, **101 first-time**; **38% of post-mortem cases involved ≥2 NPS**; bromazolam the most frequently detected designer benzodiazepine.
- **2025-11** — DEA bulletin on bromazolam: **>1,000 tablet-form exhibits**, 96% rectangular/oval with alprazolam-associated markings. NDEWS weekly briefing focuses on nitazenes.
- **2025-10** — MHRA operation near Northampton recovering ~12,000 doses of unlicensed weight-loss medicines. **(date disputed — also reported as May 2026; unverified)**
- **2025-09** — DEA state/territory reporting: nitazene threat **relatively small compared with fentanyl**.
- **2025-09** — Review published: *"Outsmarting generic legislation: 4 years into the cat-and-mouse game of the synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonist market since the Chinese ban in 2021"* ([PMID 40983778](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40983778/)). **Journal/authors unverified — PubMed unreachable from this session.**
- **2025-08-22** — H.R. 5032, **Nitazene Control Act**, introduced; would schedule the benzimidazole-opioid class permanently. No evidence of enactment.
- **2025-08-15** — DEA **temporary emergency Schedule I** order for two nitazenes on imminent-hazard grounds.
- **2025-07-09** — CFSRE/Ghent publication on ban-evading synthetic cannabinoid distribution: distributed semi-finished substances weakly CB1-active, end products strongly active — legal-status screening of shipped material does not predict consumed pharmacology.
- **2025-07-01** — China extends generic control to **benzimidazole-type synthetic cannabinoids**.
- **2025-06/07** — **China places the nitazene class under domestic control** via a generic definition anchored on etonitazene (UNODC records 6 June 2025; reported nationally as a July 2025 ban). **The EUDA later attributes the rise of orphines to this.**
- **2025-06-26** — DEA notice of intent to temporarily schedule **seven further benzimidazole-opioids**.
- **2025-06** — China schedules all **INCB-listed fentanyl precursors**.
- **2025-05-22** — 503B enforcement date for semaglutide compounding.
- **2025-04-24** — 503A grace period for compounded semaglutide ends.
- **2025-03-19** — 503B deadline to stop compounding tirzepatide.
- **2025-03-05** — FDA signals 503A enforcement discretion for tirzepatide has passed.
- **2025-02-21** — FDA declares the **semaglutide injection shortage resolved**.
- **2025** — *World Drug Report 2025*: preliminary reports from Estonia and Latvia suggest rising nitazene-linked deaths.
- **2025** — US 2019–2024 analysis: nitazene detections rising **~45% per year**; calls for prioritising validation of detection methods.

## 2024

- **2024-12-19** — FDA re-issues the determination that the **tirzepatide shortage is resolved** (after the Oct 2024 decision was remanded).
- **2024-10-02** — FDA's first tirzepatide shortage-resolved determination; subsequently remanded following litigation.
- **2024-07-29** — DEA temporary Schedule I for N-desethyl isotonitazene and N-piperidinyl etonitazene (two-year term to 2026-07-29).
- **2024** — EUDA/Europol *EU Drug Markets Analysis 2024*: **>30.7 t NPS seized in Europe in 2022**, dominated by a few cathinone and ketamine seizures; partial **production shift from China to India**.
- **2024** — Baseline year for later comparisons: ~400 NPS detected in EU seizures; five substances ≈90% of seized NPS quantity; nitazene deaths/acute toxicity reported in Germany, Sweden, Norway; first of the nine post-2024 orphines identified.
