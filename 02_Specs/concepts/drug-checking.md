# Drug checking

Fentanyl/benzo test strips and lab drug-checking services catch adulterants; a negative medetomidine strip does not rule it out. Testing menus must be updated as new analogs appear.

## Applies to
[[nitazenes]] · [[medetomidine]] · [[btmps]] · [[bromazolam]]
