## Interpretation
- Real but **modest** effect on desire/distress in a narrowly defined population; ~3 in 4 treated women did not reach the responder threshold.
- No approved or well-evidenced use in men, postmenopausal women, or as an "aphrodisiac" in people without HSDD.
- The "no increase in satisfying sexual events" finding is per FDA review summaries and widely reported; check full paper before quantitative reuse.
