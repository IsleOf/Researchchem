# PT-141 — claims vs evidence (L3)

Back to [[pt-141/index]].

| Marketing claim | What evidence shows | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "FDA-approved libido peptide" | Approved only for premenopausal women with acquired generalised HSDD (Vyleesi). Grey-market products are not that product. | **Partly true / misleading** |
| "Works for everyone, men and women" | Phase 3 data are in premenopausal women only; male ED intranasal program was halted. | **Not supported** for men |
| "Dramatically boosts desire" | ~25% vs ~17% responders; modest group mean changes; no increase in satisfying sexual events. | **Overstated** |
| "Works in the brain, not blood flow" | Consistent with MC4R mechanism and rat proceptive-behaviour data. | **Supported** (mechanistically) |
| "No side effects" | Nausea ~40%, flushing, headache, transient BP rise, possibly permanent focal hyperpigmentation, naltrexone interaction. | **False** |
| "Same as Melanotan II, also tans you" | Related scaffold; pigmentation occurs as an adverse effect, but PT-141 is not a tanning product and MT-II is unapproved. | **Misleading** |
