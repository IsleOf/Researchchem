# GH secretagogues and GHRH analogues

**Family ID:** `family:gh-secretagogues`

Compounds that raise endogenous growth hormone by acting *upstream* of the pituitary,
rather than supplying GH itself. Two receptor pathways, both converging on the
anterior-pituitary somatotroph:

| Pathway | Receptor | Node | Example in this family |
|---|---|---|---|
| GHRH / GRF | GHRH receptor, class B1 GPCR, Gs/cAMP | [[target:ghrhr]] | [[cjc-1295]] |
| Ghrelin / GHS | GHSR-1a, class A GPCR, Gq/PLC/Ca²⁺ | [[target:ghsr]] | [[ipamorelin]] |

## Members

- **[[cjc-1295]]** — long-acting GHRH analogue. Two distinct molecules share the name:
  the albumin-conjugated **DAC** form (half-life ~5.8–8.1 days) and
  **no-DAC / Mod GRF (1-29)**, which is short-acting. All human data belong to the DAC form.
  Development halted in 2006 after a participant death in phase 2.
- **[[ipamorelin]]** — selective pentapeptide ghrelin-receptor agonist, half-life ~2 h.
  Developed for postoperative ileus, not body composition; its one published phase 2 RCT
  was negative.

## The blend

The two are most often encountered **sold together** as a pre-mixed
"CJC-1295 + ipamorelin" product. The rationale — hit two receptors, two pathways, one
somatotroph — is mechanistically coherent, and it is also the entire basis of the claim.
**No published human study has tested the combination for any endpoint**, including
whether the GH or IGF-1 response is additive. Any claim about the blend is an inference
from two separately studied single agents, one of which has no outcome data and the other
of which failed its only outcome trial.

## What this family's evidence base looks like

A recurring shape, worth stating once at family level:

1. **Biomarker effects are real and well documented.** Both compounds raise GH; CJC-1295
   DAC raises IGF-1 for 9–11 days after a single dose. This part is not in dispute.
2. **Outcome evidence is absent, not merely weak.** Across both compounds there is
   **not one human trial** reporting body composition, lean mass, fat mass, strength,
   sleep, recovery, or healing. The closest long-term randomized data for the class is
   MK-677 (a different, oral molecule): 12 months increased fat-free mass but did **not**
   reduce intra-abdominal adiposity and **reduced insulin sensitivity**
   (Nass 2008, *Ann Intern Med*, PMID 18981485).
3. **Both development programmes ended badly.** CJC-1295's phase 2 was terminated after a
   participant death (causality never publicly established). Ipamorelin's phase 2 missed
   its endpoint (p = 0.15) and a later 320-patient trial completed in 2014 without
   publishing results.
4. **Shared class risks:** reduced insulin sensitivity and raised fasting glucose
   ([[risk:insulin-resistance]]); fluid retention and arthralgia
   ([[risk:water-retention]]); a theoretical neoplasia concern from sustained IGF-1
   elevation ([[risk:igf1-cancer-theoretical]]) resting on observational epidemiology
   (Renehan 2004, *Lancet*, PMID 15110491) with **no carcinogenicity study of either
   compound**. A 2018 class review found GH secretagogues generally well tolerated but
   named glucose and long-term cancer/mortality as the open questions
   (Sigalos & Pastuszak, *Sex Med Rev* 2018;6(1):45-53, PMID 28400207).
   Neither compound suppresses the HPG axis.
5. **Ipamorelin carries an extra, mechanism-intrinsic one:** GHSR-1a is the ghrelin
   receptor, so appetite stimulation and weight gain are on-target effects
   ([[risk:appetite-stimulation]]).

## Regulatory position (both members)

- **Not approved** as medicines in any region; no USP/NF monograph for either.
- **US compounding:** both were placed in FDA **Category 2** (bulk substances raising
  *significant safety risks*); both were removed from Category 2 in 2024 for procedural
  reasons, **not as a safety clearance**; both went to FDA's Pharmacy Compounding Advisory
  Committee (ipamorelin October 2024, CJC-1295 December 2024) where FDA proposed/assessed
  **against** 503A bulks-list inclusion. Neither is on the 503A list.
- **WADA:** both **prohibited at all times under Section S2** and both named explicitly —
  CJC-1295 under GHRH analogues, ipamorelin under growth hormone secretagogues.
