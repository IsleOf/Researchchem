# Pinealon (EDR)

**Family:** [[khavinson-bioregulators]]

## Summary
Pinealon is a synthetic tripeptide, Glu-Asp-Arg (EDR), from the Khavinson bioregulator programme. It is promoted as a "brain" or neuroprotective peptide for cognition, memory and recovery after brain injury.

## Status at a glance
- **Approval:** Not approved by any stringent regulator.
- **US compounding:** No FDA bulks-list record found; secondary sources claim Category 2, unverified.
- **WADA:** Likely S0 (non-approved substance). See [[pinealon/regulatory]].

## Evidence tier: 3 (preclinical only; human data unverifiable)
- **Preclinical evidence:**
  - Rat prenatal hyperhomocysteinaemia model.
  - Cell ROS and viability studies.
  - Mouse hippocampal neuron cultures and 5xFAD mice (dendritic spines).
- **Human evidence:**
  - Claims of a 72-patient post-traumatic "cerebrasthenia" trial and a 32-patient open-label cohort circulate on vendor and secondary sites.
  - No primary indexed record was found.
- **Quality concern:** All primary studies are from the Khavinson group and collaborators. No independent replication was found.
