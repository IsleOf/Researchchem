# Pinealon: mechanism (pharmacology only)

Up: [[pinealon/index]]

| Proposed action | Basis | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **Antioxidant / ROS suppression, reduced necrotic cell death** | Cerebellar granule cells, neutrophils, PC12 cells ([PMID 21978084](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21978084/), Rejuvenation Res 2011;14(5):535-41). Rat offspring neurons ([PMID 22567179](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22567179/)) | In vitro / ex vivo; single group |
| **Delayed ERK1/2 activation, cell-cycle modification** | Same 2011 paper | In vitro |
| **Dendritic (mushroom) spine preservation under amyloid toxicity** | Mouse hippocampal cultures: EDR raised mushroom spines by 71% ([PMID 28853087](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28853087/)). 5xFAD mice (Pharmaceuticals 2021, [PMC8227791](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8227791/)) | Preclinical |
| **Histone / DNA binding; promoter binding (CASP3, APOE, SOD2), gene-expression modulation** | Developer review ([PMC7795577](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7795577), Molecules 2021;26(1):159); systematic review ([PMID 34834147](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34834147/)) | Hypothesis / in silico docking |
| **Pathways listed:** MAPK/ERK, caspase-3, p53, SOD2, GPX1, PPARA/PPARG, serotonin, calmodulin | Same review | Not independently confirmed |
