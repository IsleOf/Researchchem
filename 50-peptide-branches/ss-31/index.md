# SS-31 / elamipretide (L1)

Part of [[targeted-therapeutic-peptides]]. Also called MTP-131, Bendavia; marketed in the US as **Forzinity** (elamipretide HCl).

**What it is:** a synthetic, cell-permeable aromatic-cationic tetrapeptide (D-Arg-Dmt-Lys-Phe-NH2) that concentrates in the inner mitochondrial membrane and binds cardiolipin. Developed by Stealth BioTherapeutics from Szeto–Schiller (SS) peptide work.

**Status (2026-11):** FDA **accelerated approval 19 Sep 2025** (Forzinity) to improve muscle strength in Barth syndrome patients weighing ≥30 kg — the first FDA-approved therapy for Barth syndrome and first approved mitochondria-targeted drug. Approval rests on a surrogate (knee-extensor strength); a confirmatory RCT is required. Followed a Complete Response Letter in May 2025. Not approved for any other indication; investigational elsewhere.

**Evidence tier: 2** (scale used in this family: 1 = replicated phase 3 RCT/full approval; 2 = small or mixed human RCTs / surrogate-based approval; 3 = preclinical or anecdotal only). Human evidence is mixed: several large trials missed primary endpoints (MMPOWER-3, EMBRACE STEMI, ReCLAIM-2), and the Barth approval rests on a 12-patient crossover with open-label extension data.
