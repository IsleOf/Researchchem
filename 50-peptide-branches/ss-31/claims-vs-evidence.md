# SS-31 — claims vs evidence (L3)

Back to [[ss-31/index]].

| Marketing claim | What evidence shows | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "FDA-approved mitochondrial peptide" | True only for Forzinity in Barth syndrome (≥30 kg), under accelerated approval on a surrogate endpoint. Not approved for any other use; grey-market vials are not the approved product. | **Misleading** when used to imply general approval |
| "Boosts energy / reverses fatigue" | MMPOWER-3 (n=218) showed **no** improvement in fatigue or 6MWT in mitochondrial myopathy; TAZPOWER double-blind phase also missed primary endpoints. | **Not supported** |
| "Anti-aging / longevity" | Aged-mouse data (Chiao 2020) show reversal of diastolic dysfunction; no human aging data. | **Preclinical only** |
| "Heart protection" | EMBRACE STEMI negative for infarct size; HF trials small/inconclusive. | **Not supported** in humans |
| "Improves vision / AMD" | ReCLAIM-2 missed primary endpoints; secondary EZ-loss signals being tested in phase 3. | **Unproven / under study** |
| "Safe — only injection site reactions" | Consistent with trial data in disease populations; long-term data limited; unregulated products add contamination risk. | **Partly supported** (for pharmaceutical product only) |
