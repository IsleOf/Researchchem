# SS-31 — human evidence (L2)

Back to [[ss-31/index]]. Overall: **mixed**. Several adequately sized RCTs missed their primary endpoints; positive signals are mostly secondary, post hoc, or open-label.

| Study | Year | n | Design | Outcome | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EMBRACE STEMI (Gibson et al., Eur Heart J 37:1296) | 2016 | 297 | Phase 2a RCT, IV MTP-131 vs placebo during primary PCI for anterior STEMI | **Negative** primary (infarct size by CK-MB AUC not reduced). Exploratory: less early new-onset HF. Safe. | https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehv597 · NCT01572909 |
| Daubert et al., Circ Heart Fail | 2017 | ~36 (3 dose cohorts) | Phase 1/2 RCT, single 4-h IV infusion, HFrEF (EF ≤35%) | Safe/tolerated; transient LV volume changes at highest dose; PK short. Hypothesis-generating only. | https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCHEARTFAILURE.117.004389 |

## Interpretation
- The only approval rests on a very small trial whose randomised phase missed its primaries; FDA used an intermediate endpoint under accelerated approval.
- No human evidence for "anti-aging", energy, cognition or athletic performance in healthy people.
