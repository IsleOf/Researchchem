## US 503A compounding timeline
- **Category 2:** FDA's "Safety Risks Associated with Certain Bulk Drug Substances" page listed epitalon. FDA stated it lacked sufficient safety information to know whether it would cause harm in humans.
- **2026:** FDA announced its intent to remove 12 peptides, including epitalon, from Category 2 ([Frier Levitt](https://www.frierlevitt.com/articles/fda-peptides-do-not-compound-list-update-2026/)).
- **24 Jul 2026 PCAC vote:** The committee voted **7–4 (1 abstention)** to recommend adding epitalon (free base and acetate) to the 503A Bulks List. FDA staff had recommended against ([AJMC](https://ajmc.com/view/fda-panel-backs-6-peptides-for-compounding); [Orrick](https://www.orrick.com/en/Insights/2026/07/FDA-Peptide-Compounding-Vote-What-to-Watch-at-the-July-PCAC-Meeting)).
- **Effect:** The vote is **advisory only**. Lawful 503A compounding requires FDA rulemaking, which is not complete as of this note.
