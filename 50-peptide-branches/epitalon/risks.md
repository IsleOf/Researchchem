# Epitalon: risks

Up: [[epitalon/index]]

> **Not found:** Formal Phase 1, dose-escalation or pharmacovigilance data. Absence of reported harm ≠ evidence of safety.

## Known or reported
- **Injection-site reactions:** Redness or irritation is reported anecdotally and in secondary sources. No systematic data.
- **Headache, sleep changes, vivid dreams:** Anecdotal and secondary-source reports only.

## Theoretical and serious
- **`risk:theoretical-oncogenic` (telomere maintenance):**
  - Telomerase reactivation is a hallmark of most cancers.
  - Al-Dulaimi 2025 found Epitalon lengthened telomeres in breast-cancer cell lines via ALT ([doi:10.1007/s10522-025-10315-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10522-025-10315-x)).
  - Some rodent data show fewer tumours (Anisimov 2003), so the direction in vivo is unknown.
  - People with a current or past cancer or precancerous conditions face the largest unknown.
- **`risk:no-human-safety-data`:**
  - No human pharmacokinetics.
  - No interaction studies.
  - No pregnancy or lactation data.
  - No paediatric data.
- **`risk:unregulated-product-quality`:**
  - Sold mostly as "research chemical" grey-market product.
  - Identity, purity, sterility and endotoxin content are unverified.
  - Non-sterile injectables carry infection and abscess risk. That risk applies to the product route, not the molecule.

## Hormonal
- **Pineal effects:** It is claimed to modulate pineal and melatonin function, with conflicting data. Possible additive effects with melatonin or sedatives are theoretical and unstudied.
- **HPG axis:** No evidence of HPG-axis suppression was found, but it has not been studied.

## Contraindications (precautionary, not evidence-derived)
- Active or past malignancy.
- Pregnancy and breastfeeding.
- Competitive athletes (see [[epitalon/regulatory]]).
