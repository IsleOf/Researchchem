# Epitalon: mechanism (pharmacology only)

Up: [[epitalon/index]]

> The 2025 review by Araj et al. states that the precise mechanism "remains unverified" ([PMC11943447](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11943447/), doi:10.3390/ijms26062691). Every pathway below is a proposed one.

## Proposed targets and pathways

| Proposed action | Basis | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **Telomerase / hTERT upregulation → telomere elongation** | Khavinson 2003, human fetal fibroblasts ([PMID 12937682](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12937682/)). Al-Dulaimi 2025, normal epithelial and fibroblast lines: hTERT mRNA↑, telomerase activity↑ ([doi:10.1007/s10522-025-10315-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10522-025-10315-x)) | In vitro only. One independent replication. |
| **ALT (alternative lengthening of telomeres) activation in cancer cells** | Al-Dulaimi 2025 (21NT, BT474 breast cancer lines). A correction replaced figures, including the ALT figure ([doi:10.1007/s10522-025-10326-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10522-025-10326-8)) | In vitro. Safety-relevant (see [[epitalon/risks]]). |
| **Chromatin decondensation (deheterochromatinization)** | Lezhava/Khavinson, lymphocytes from elderly donors ([PMID 16705247](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16705247/)) | Small; single group. |
| **Short-peptide–DNA/histone binding ("epigenetic" regulation)** | Khavinson group systematic review ([PMID 34834147](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34834147/)); AEDG and neurogenesis gene expression in gingival MSCs (Molecules 2020, 25:609) | Largely hypothesis plus docking. Developer-authored. |
| **Pineal / melatonin rhythm normalisation** | Melatonin data are mostly for Epithalamin (extract) in elderly people (Korkushko 2004, Bull Exp Biol Med 137:389). The Araj review notes a study finding Epitalon did *not* change pineal melatonin secretion. | Conflicting. |
| **Antioxidant effects** | Group reports, summarised in the Araj 2025 review | Weak. |

## Pharmacokinetics
- No published human pharmacokinetic study was found: no half-life, bioavailability or distribution data.
- As an unmodified tetrapeptide, it would be expected to undergo rapid peptidase degradation. That is an inference, not a measurement.
- How a 4-residue peptide reaches the nucleus in vivo at meaningful concentrations is unexplained.
