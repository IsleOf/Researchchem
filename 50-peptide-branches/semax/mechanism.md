# Semax: mechanism (pharmacology only)

## Structure
Met-Glu-His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro. Compared with native ACTH(4-10) (Met-Glu-His-Phe-Arg-Trp-Gly), the C-terminal Arg-Trp-Gly is replaced by **Pro-Gly-Pro**, a sequence that resists proteolysis. The ACTH(4-7) core carries the "behavioural" activity of ACTH fragments without steroidogenic (adrenal) activity.

## Proposed targets and pathways
| Target / pathway | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **BDNF / TrkB (`target:trkb`)**: higher exon III BDNF and TrkB mRNA in rat hippocampus, plus improved conditioned avoidance learning | Dolotov et al. 2006, Brain Res ([PMID 16996037](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16996037/)) | Rat only; one group |
| **Neurotrophin gene transcription after ischemia**: Semax and its metabolite PGP activate neurotrophin and neurotrophin-receptor genes | 2009 rat study ([PMID 19633950](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19633950/)) | Rat only |
| **Immune/vascular gene expression after focal ischemia**: >50 % of the genes Semax altered were immune-response genes (immunoglobulins, chemokines); vascular-development genes were also affected | Medvedeva et al. 2014, BMC Genomics ([PMID 24661604](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24661604/), [doi:10.1186/1471-2164-15-228](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2164-15-228)); 2017 follow-up ([PMID 28255762](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28255762/), [doi:10.1007/s00438-017-1297-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00438-017-1297-1)) | Rat only |
| **Melanocortin receptors MC4R/MC5R (`target:mc4r`, `target:mc5r`)**: reported competitive antagonism of α-MSH at MC4/MC5 but not MC3 | Usually attributed to Adan et al. 1994, Eur J Pharmacol ([PMID 7895772](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7895772/)), which tested the chemically identical [Pro8,10,Gly9]ACTH(4-10), and/or a 2012 review (Bertolini, [PMID 22293371](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22293371/)) | **Attribution not verified against the full text**; in vitro |
| **Enkephalinase inhibition (`target:enkephalinase`)**: inhibits enkephalin-degrading enzymes of human serum (IC50 ≈ 10 µM) | Kost et al. 2001, Bioorg Khim ([PMID 11443939](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11443939/)) | In vitro; µM range (low potency) |

The overall mechanism is **unresolved**. No high-affinity receptor that explains the clinical claims has been established.

## Kinetics (no dosing)
- In rats given labelled Semax intranasally, the brain held about 0.093 % of administered radioactivity per gram at 2 min. Roughly 80 % of that was intact peptide. Semax is broken down rapidly, and **Pro-Gly-Pro** is the main fragment found in biological samples. In vitro proteolysis gives His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro, Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro and PGP. (Shevchenko/Myasoedov group, Russ J Bioorg Chem 2006; [catalogue record](https://katalog.lib.cas.cz/KNAV/EdsRecord/cmedm,16523722); PMID not directly verified.)
- **Half-life:** no primary human half-life value was found. The "minutes" figures on hobbyist sites are uncited. Because the metabolites (e.g., PGP) are themselves reported to be active, parent-drug half-life may not track the effect.
