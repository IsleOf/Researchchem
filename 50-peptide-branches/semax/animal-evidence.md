# Semax: animal and cell evidence

| Year | Species | Model | Outcome | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2006 | Rat | Intranasal Semax; hippocampal gene expression + conditioned avoidance learning | ↑ exon III BDNF and TrkB mRNA; more conditioned avoidance reactions | [PMID 16996037](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16996037/) |
| 2009 | Rat | Cerebral ischemia; Semax and PGP | Activated transcription of neurotrophins and their receptor genes | [PMID 19633950](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19633950/) |
| 2014 | Rat | Permanent MCAO (focal ischemia); genome-wide transcriptome of cortex | Mostly ↑ immune-response genes (immunoglobulins, chemokines) at 24 h; vascular-development genes changed | [PMID 24661604](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24661604/) |
| 2017 | Rat | Focal ischemia; immune-response genes | ↑ immunoglobulin heavy-chain gene; effects on cytokine, stress-response and ribosomal-protein genes | [PMID 28255762](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28255762/) |
| 2006 | Rat | Intranasal labelled Semax; brain/blood kinetics | Rapid brain entry; rapid degradation to PGP and other fragments | [catalogue record](https://katalog.lib.cas.cz/KNAV/EdsRecord/cmedm,16523722) |
| 1994 | In vitro (cloned MC receptors) | [Pro8,10,Gly9]ACTH(4-10) (= Semax) at MC3/4/5 | Reported MC4/MC5 antagonism (attribution not verified) | [PMID 7895772](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7895772/) |
| 2001 | In vitro (human serum) | Enkephalin-degrading enzymes | Inhibition, IC50 ≈ 10 µM | [PMID 11443939](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11443939/) |

**Caveats:** almost all of this comes from one institute. Gene-expression readouts are not functional outcomes. A 2021 RNA-seq/qRT-PCR follow-up ([PMC8226508](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8226508); matched from search, not read in full) suggests Semax *suppresses* ischemia-induced pro-inflammatory gene induction, which sits uneasily with the 2014 "immune genes up" framing. The direction depends on the time point and the comparison.
