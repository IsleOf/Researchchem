# Semax: human evidence

**Overall:** small studies, nearly all from one Moscow clinical group (Gusev, Skvortsova and colleagues) and the Institute of Molecular Genetics, published in Russian. No large, independently replicated, randomised placebo-controlled trial was found. **No human study outside Russia/post-Soviet states was found.**

| Year | n | Design | Population | Outcome | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1997 | 30 treated vs 80 controls | **Non-randomised**, controlled with conventional therapy; clinical + EEG mapping + somatosensory evoked potentials | Acute hemispheric ischemic stroke | Reported faster recovery of neurological (mainly motor) function; abstract gives no effect sizes or p-values | [PMID 11517472](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11517472/) |
| 2018 | 110 | Clinical study of Semax during rehabilitation at different stages after stroke (design details not in English abstract) | Post-ischemic-stroke rehab | Associated with higher plasma BDNF and better Barthel index scores | [PMID 29798983](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29798983/) |
| 2018 | 24 (14 Semax / 10 placebo) | Placebo-controlled resting-state fMRI before and at 5 and 20 min after intranasal dosing | Healthy volunteers, mean age ~44 | Larger rostral default-mode-network (medial frontal) component in the Semax group; imaging biomarker only, no cognitive outcome | [PMID 30225715](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30225715/) |
| 2020 | small (not given in abstract) | Resting-state fMRI connectomics: Semax vs Selank vs placebo | Healthy volunteers | Differences in connectivity between the right amygdala and temporal/parahippocampal regions | [PMID 32342318](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32342318/) |
| ~1996 | ~11 (reported) | Healthy operators on work shifts; attention/short-term memory and EEG | Healthy adults | Reported "nootropic-like" EEG changes and better attention. **Primary record not verified**; known only via a Russian abstract index ([istina](https://istina.pskgu.ru/publications/article/2237320)) and secondary summaries | n/a |

## Not found / gaps
- No RCT with a hard clinical endpoint (mortality, mRS disability at 90 days) in stroke.
- No human trials for the popular claims (ADHD, healthy-adult cognition, depression, anxiety). A Russian paediatric ADHD pilot is mentioned on secondary sites but **could not be located**.
- Optic-nerve-atrophy use (Russian ophthalmology) rests on small open studies. These were cited secondarily and **not verified**.
- No pharmacokinetic study in humans was found.
