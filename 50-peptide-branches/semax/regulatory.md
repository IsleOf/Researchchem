# Semax: regulatory status

| Region | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Russia** | Registered medicine (intranasal 0.1 % and 1 % solutions). Indications reported as ischemic stroke/TIA, cognitive and cerebrovascular disorders and optic-nerve disease. It is reportedly included on the Russian List of Vital & Essential Drugs (2011). | Secondary sources only; the official register (grls.minzdrav.gov.ru) was **not checked directly** |
| **USA (FDA)** | Not an approved drug. In 2023 FDA listed "Semax (heptapeptide)" in **503A bulk drug substances Category 2** (significant safety risks), and it remained there in 2024. Reports on its 2026 status conflict: some cite a planned compounding advisory review and others a withdrawn nomination. **Verify on FDA's 503A page.** | [FDA categories list](https://fda.gov/media/94155/download); [A4PC 2023 summary](https://a4pc.org/files/503A-Categories-Update-for-September-2023-FINAL.docx.pdf) |
| **EU / UK / Australia / Canada** | No marketing authorisation found | Absence of evidence (no approval located) |
| **WADA** | Not named on the Prohibited List. Some bodies (e.g., BSCG) say S0 "non-approved substances" *could* cover it. S0 applies only to substances not approved by **any** governmental health authority, and Semax is approved in Russia, so S0 coverage is **ambiguous**. It could still be judged under other classes, so athletes should seek a ruling from their anti-doping organisation. | [DSHS Köln S0 explainer](https://www.dshs-koeln.de/en/universitaet/organisation/institute-einrichtungen/department-of-biochemistry/doping-substances/prohibited-classes-of-substances-and-methods/s0-active-ingredients-not-approved-for-medical-use); [BSCG](https://www.bscg.org/wada-prohibited-list-banned-drugs-and-supplement-risks); [USADA 2025 list changes](https://www.usada.org/spirit-of-sport/new-2025-wada-prohibited-list/) |
