# Prostamax: mechanism (pharmacology only)

Up: [[prostamax/index]]

| Proposed action | Basis | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **Chromatin decondensation / "deheterochromatinization"** | Lymphocytes from donors aged 75–86: fewer pericentromeric heterochromatin segments on chromosomes 1 and 9, more Ag-positive nucleolar organiser regions, higher sister chromatid exchange frequency ([PMID 23221144](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23221144/), Georgian Med News 2012, Russian). Leukocytes from people aged 75–88 (Khavinson, Lezhava, Malinin, Bull Exp Biol Med 2004, [doi:10.1023/B:BEBM.0000024393.40560.05](https://doi.org/10.1023/B:BEBM.0000024393.40560.05)) | Small; one group |
| **Anti-inflammatory / anti-sclerotic effects in prostate tissue** | Rat chronic aseptic prostatitis (Borovskaya 2013, [doi:10.4236/mri.2013.23007](https://doi.org/10.4236/mri.2013.23007)) | Single animal study |
| **Tissue-specific gene regulation via DNA/histone binding** | The group's general short-peptide hypothesis ([PMID 34834147](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34834147/)) | Speculative |

## Pharmacokinetics
- No PK data were found in any species.
- No structural data (NMR, crystal) for KEDP bound to DNA were found.

## Interpretation caution
- The SCE increase is a marker conventionally associated with genotoxic stress. The developers instead frame "chromatin decondensation" as gene reactivation, and that framing is not independently established.
