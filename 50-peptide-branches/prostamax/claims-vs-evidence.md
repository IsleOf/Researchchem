# Prostamax: claims vs evidence

Up: [[prostamax/index]]

| Marketing claim | What the evidence shows | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "Treats chronic prostatitis" | One rat aseptic prostatitis study (Borovskaya 2013). No human trials. | **Preclinical only** |
| "Shrinks the prostate / treats BPH" | No BPH model or human data found | **No evidence** |
| "Restores youthful gene expression / reverses chromatin ageing" | Small in vitro lymphocyte cytogenetics (2004, 2012) from the developer group; functional relevance unknown | **Speculative** |
| "Improves sexual function / urinary flow" | No data found | **No evidence** |
| "Prevents prostate cancer" | No data. Theoretical concern about chromatin effects and delayed diagnosis. | **Unsupported** |
| "Clinically tested in Russia" | No indexed human study located | **Unverified** |

**Quality flag:** The evidence base is the thinnest in the family. All studies are from the Khavinson network.
