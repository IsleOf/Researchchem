# Prostamax: risks

Up: [[prostamax/index]]

> **Not found:** Human safety, toxicology or interaction data. Risk is **unknown**.

## Theoretical
- **`risk:no-human-safety-data`:** Nothing is known about adverse-event rates or interactions.
- **`risk:delayed-diagnosis`:**
  - Using an unproven peptide for prostate symptoms may delay evaluation of BPH, infection or **prostate cancer**.
  - PSA testing, imaging and urology review remain the standard.
- **Genome / chromatin effects:**
  - In vitro "heterochromatin reactivation" and raised sister chromatid exchange are of unknown significance.
  - Their relevance to neoplasia, including in a prostate with occult cancer, is unstudied.
- **`risk:unregulated-product-quality`:** Grey-market identity, purity, sterility and endotoxin content are unverified.

## Hormonal
- No data on testosterone, DHT, PSA or HPG-axis effects.

## Precautionary contraindications
- Known or suspected prostate cancer.
- Undiagnosed urinary symptoms.
- Competitive athletes (see [[prostamax/regulatory]]).
