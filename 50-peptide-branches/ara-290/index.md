# ARA-290 / cibinetide (L1)

Part of [[targeted-therapeutic-peptides]]. Also: pyroglutamate helix B surface peptide (pHBSP). Developer: Araim Pharmaceuticals.

**What it is:** an 11-amino-acid peptide (reported sequence pGlu-EQLERALNSS) modelled on the aqueous face of helix B of erythropoietin (EPO). It is designed to activate the tissue-protective **innate repair receptor** (EPOR/CD131 heteromer) **without** stimulating red-cell production via the EPOR homodimer.

**Status (2026-11):** Investigational; **not approved anywhere**. EU orphan designation EU/3/13/1191 (sarcoidosis, 2013); EMA notes a US orphan designation for neuropathic pain due to sarcoidosis. No phase 3 trial found; development appears stalled (unverified).

**Evidence tier: 2** — a handful of small (n≈22–64), short (28-day) placebo-controlled phase 2 trials, mostly by the same investigator/sponsor group, with surrogate endpoints (corneal nerve fibre metrics) and patient-reported symptoms.
