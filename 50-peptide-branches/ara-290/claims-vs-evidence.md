# ARA-290 — claims vs evidence (L3)

Back to [[ara-290/index]].

| Marketing claim | What evidence shows | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "Regenerates nerves / cures neuropathy" | Small 28-day RCTs show increased corneal/skin small-fibre measures and some symptom improvement in sarcoidosis and T2D neuropathy. Surrogate endpoints; no phase 3. | **Preliminary, overstated** |
| "EPO benefits without raising red cells" | Non-erythropoietic in animals (Brines 2008); no haematocrit signal reported in trials. Tissue-protective benefits in humans are unproven outside neuropathy. | **Partly supported** (mechanistic) |
| "Heals injuries, kidney, heart, brain" | Rodent models only (stroke, renal I/R, cardiomyocytes). | **Preclinical only** |
| "Improves blood sugar" | HbA1c/lipid improvement reported in one small T2D trial; not replicated. | **Weak** |
| "Safe, no side effects" | No signal in short small trials; long-term safety unknown; unregulated products add risk. | **Unknown** |
| "Not banned in sport" | Investigational substances are prohibited under WADA S0. | **False** (by S0 definition) |
