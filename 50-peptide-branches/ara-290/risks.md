# ARA-290 — risks (L2)

Back to [[ara-290/index]].

## Reported in trials
- No safety signal in small 28-day trials (Heij 2012; Brines 2015 "no potential safety issues identified"). Sample sizes (total low hundreds) are far too small to detect uncommon harms.
- No haematocrit rise reported — consistent with non-erythropoietic design (`effect:non-erythropoietic`).

## Theoretical / unknown
- **Long-term safety unknown** (`risk:unknown-long-term-safety`): no exposure beyond weeks in controlled trials.
- EPO-pathway relation: EPO itself carries thrombotic and tumour-progression concerns; ARA-290 is designed to avoid the EPOR homodimer, but whether IRR activation affects tumour biology in humans is unstudied (theoretical; not observed).
- Immunogenicity of a synthetic peptide: not characterised in published human data.
- Injection-related risks; with unregulated "research" products: identity/purity/sterility/endotoxin unverified (`risk:product-quality`).
- No hormone-axis suppression expected or reported.

Sources: Heij 2012; Brines 2015 (PMC4365069); Brines 2008 (PMID 18676614).
