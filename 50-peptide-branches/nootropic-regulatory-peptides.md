# Nootropic / regulatory peptides (Russian-approved)

Short synthetic heptapeptides developed at the Institute of Molecular Genetics (Russian Academy of Sciences). Both use the same stabilising design: a native regulatory peptide fragment extended with a C-terminal **Pro-Gly-Pro (PGP)** tripeptide to slow enzymatic breakdown. Both are given intranasally in the Russian clinical literature.

| Compound | Parent peptide | Main proposed actions | Status | Evidence tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[semax/index\|Semax]] | ACTH(4-10) (Met-Glu-His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro) | BDNF/TrkB upregulation, melanocortin-receptor modulation, neuroprotective gene expression after ischemia | Registered in Russia (stroke/cognitive indications); not approved by FDA/EMA | 3 |
| [[selank/index\|Selank]] | Tuftsin (Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg-Pro-Gly-Pro) | GABA-A allosteric modulation, enkephalinase inhibition, immunomodulation | Registered in Russia (anxiolytic) per secondary sources; not approved by FDA/EMA | 3 |

Evidence tier scale used in this family: **1** = replicated, independent RCTs; **2** = at least one well-reported RCT or consistent controlled human data; **3** = mostly preclinical plus small, single-group or non-randomised human studies.

## Shared themes
- **One research ecosystem.** Almost all data (animal and human) come from a small number of Moscow groups and are published mainly in Russian-language journals. No independent Western RCT was found for either compound.
- **Shared mechanism node:** both inhibit enkephalin-degrading enzymes of human serum in vitro (Kost et al. 2001, [PMID 11443939](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11443939/)).
- **Shared human imaging study:** resting-state fMRI connectivity study comparing Semax, Selank and placebo (Panikratova et al. 2020, [PMID 32342318](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32342318/)).
- **Anti-doping:** neither is named on the WADA Prohibited List; athletes' bodies (e.g., BSCG summaries) treat them as possible S0 "non-approved substances", but the S0 wording excludes substances approved by *any* government health authority, and both are approved in Russia - status is ambiguous. See each `regulatory.md`.
- **US compounding:** FDA placed Semax (and, briefly, Selank acetate) in 503A bulk-substance Category 2 (significant safety risk) in 2023; Selank was later removed because the nomination was withdrawn/misdirected.
