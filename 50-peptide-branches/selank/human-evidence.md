# Selank: human evidence

**Overall:** very limited. All studies are Russian, from the developer's network. **No placebo-controlled RCT and no study outside Russia was found.**

| Year | n | Design | Population | Outcome | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2008 | 62 (30 Selank / 32 medazepam) | Randomised comparative trial vs **medazepam** (a benzodiazepine); **no placebo arm**. Secondary sources describe it as open-label and 14 days long (not verified) | Generalised anxiety disorder and neurasthenia | Anxiolytic effect reported as comparable to medazepam. Selank prolonged serum leu-enkephalin half-life, which correlated with anxiety level, mainly in GAD. The English abstract gives no HAM-A deltas or p-values | [PMID 18454096](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18454096/) |
| 2008 | same cohort (companion paper) | Ex vivo immune measures | Anxiety-asthenic disorders | Reported immunomodulatory effects (cytokine changes) | [PMID 18577961](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18577961/) |
| 2020 | small (not given in abstract) | Resting-state fMRI connectomics: Selank vs Semax vs placebo | Healthy volunteers | Changed connectivity between the right amygdala and temporal/parahippocampal regions; imaging biomarker only | [PMID 32342318](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32342318/) |

## Not found / gaps
- No placebo-controlled anxiety RCT. The widely quoted "62-patient placebo-controlled trial with 38 % vs 20 % HAM-A reduction" **could not be traced** to a primary source. The indexed 62-patient trial used medazepam as comparator.
- No trials for cognition, depression, immune or antiviral uses in humans.
- No data on long-term use, dependence or withdrawal. Claims of "no dependence" are untested in humans.
