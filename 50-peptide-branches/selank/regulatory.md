# Selank: regulatory status

| Region | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Russia** | Registered as an intranasal anxiolytic. Secondary sources report a 2009 registration for GAD and neurasthenia, but give inconsistent dates (2002 vs 2009). | Secondary sources only; the official register (grls.minzdrav.gov.ru) was **not checked directly** |
| **USA (FDA)** | Not an approved drug. "Selank acetate (TP-7)" was briefly listed in 503A Category 2 (significant safety risk) in 2023. FDA's 2024 update **removed** it because the nomination was withdrawn or the nominator meant a different substance. It is therefore not on an active 503A bulks list; its 2026 status is unclear. | [FDA categories list](https://fda.gov/media/94155/download); [A4PC 2023 summary](https://a4pc.org/files/503A-Categories-Update-for-September-2023-FINAL.docx.pdf) |
| **EU / UK / Australia / Canada** | No marketing authorisation found | Absence of evidence |
| **WADA** | Not named on the Prohibited List. It is often described as possibly falling under S0, but S0 excludes substances approved by any government health authority, and Selank is approved in Russia, so S0 coverage is **ambiguous**. Athletes should seek a ruling from their anti-doping organisation. | [DSHS Köln S0 explainer](https://www.dshs-koeln.de/en/universitaet/organisation/institute-einrichtungen/department-of-biochemistry/doping-substances/prohibited-classes-of-substances-and-methods/s0-active-ingredients-not-approved-for-medical-use); [BSCG](https://www.bscg.org/wada-prohibited-list-banned-drugs-and-supplement-risks) |
