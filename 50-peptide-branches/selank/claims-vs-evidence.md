# Selank: claims vs evidence

| Marketing claim | What the evidence shows | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "Clinically proven anxiolytic, beats placebo" | One 62-patient Russian trial against **medazepam**, with no placebo arm ([PMID 18454096](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18454096/)). The placebo figures quoted online could not be traced. | **Weak; placebo superiority unproven** |
| "As effective as benzodiazepines without sedation or dependence" | Comparable effect to medazepam in one small trial. Sedation and dependence were not systematically assessed, and there are no long-term data. | **Partly supported (efficacy, weak); safety claim unsupported** |
| "Works through GABA like benzos" | Allosteric modulation of [3H]GABA binding and GABA-like gene-expression profiles in rats and cells | **Supported preclinically; mechanism is not the benzodiazepine site per se** |
| "Boosts enkephalins / natural opioids" | Inhibits serum enkephalinases at µM concentrations; prolongs leu-enkephalin half-life in mice and patients | **Supported (modest, low potency)** |
| "Nootropic / improves memory" | Only rodent data, strain-dependent (BALB/c) | **Not supported in humans** |
| "Immune booster / antiviral" | Cell and mouse influenza data; ex vivo cytokine changes with inconsistent IL-6 direction | **Preliminary; not supported in humans** |
| "Legal / WADA-safe" | Not named, but S0 status is ambiguous; not approved in the US/EU | **Misleading** |
