# Selank: animal and cell evidence

| Year | Species | Model | Outcome | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ~2002 | Mouse (BALB/c vs C57BL/6) | Open field / anxiety tests; inbred strains with different emotional-stress phenotypes | Anxiolytic in high-anxiety BALB/c, absent in C57BL/6; ↑ plasma leu-enkephalin half-life | [doi:10.1023/A:1015582302311](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1015582302311) (bibliographic details not fully verified) |
| ~2003 | Rodents | Review: Selank and tuftsin-family peptides in adaptive behaviour under stress | Antistress, positive emotional effects | [PMID 14969422](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14969422/) |
| 2012 | Mouse (BALB/c) | Naloxone pretreatment | Naloxone attenuated Selank's effect, implicating the opioid system | Secondary summary ([aminer](https://aminer.org/pub/55a4af6365ceb7cb02d5fd6c/the-role-of-opioid-system-in-peculiarities-of-anti-anxiety-effect-of)); primary not verified |
| 2016 | Rat | Intranasal Selank vs GABA; 84-gene neurotransmission panel, frontal cortex | 45 genes changed at 1 h, 22 at 3 h; correlation with GABA profile | [doi:10.3389/fphar.2016.00031](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphar.2016.00031) |
| 2017 | Human neuroblastoma cells (IMR-32) | GABA ± Selank ± olanzapine | Selank given with GABA almost fully suppressed GABA-induced gene changes | [PMID 28293190](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28293190/) |
| 2018 | Rodent brain membranes | [3H]GABA radioligand binding | Positive allosteric modulation; interaction with benzodiazepines | [doi:10.2174/0929866525666180925144642](https://doi.org/10.2174/0929866525666180925144642) |
| 2001 | In vitro (human serum) | Enkephalin-degrading enzymes | IC50 ≈ 20 µM | [PMID 11443939](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11443939/) |
| 2009 | Cell culture + mice | Influenza A/H3N2 | Suppressed viral replication in cells when given 24 h before infection; ↑ IFN-β gene expression in mice | [Vopr Virusol record](https://virusjour.crie.ru/jour/article/view/11942) |
| 2011 | Mouse spleen | Gene expression after Selank and its fragments | Changed chemokine/cytokine and receptor gene expression at 6 and 24 h | [RUDN record](https://repository.rudn.ru/en/records/article/record/156052) |

**Caveats:** strain-dependent effects, mRNA-level readouts and a single research network. The antiviral data are preliminary and have not been replicated.
