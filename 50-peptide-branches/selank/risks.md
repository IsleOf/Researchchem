# Selank: risks

**Overall:** the human safety data are essentially one 62-patient short-term trial. No systematic adverse-event data, long-term data or pregnancy data were found. **Unknown ≠ safe.**

| Risk | Evidence | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `risk:unknown-long-term`: no long-term exposure data | Absence of studies | Includes unstudied dependence/withdrawal potential, despite marketing claims of "no dependence" |
| `risk:gabaergic-interaction`: possible interaction with benzodiazepines, alcohol and other GABA-ergic sedatives | Mechanistic only: Selank modulates [3H]GABA binding, and the combination with benzodiazepines is "not cumulative" ([doi:10.2174/0929866525666180925144642](https://doi.org/10.2174/0929866525666180925144642)) | Theoretical; not studied clinically |
| `risk:opioid-interaction`: enkephalinase inhibition and naloxone-sensitive effects | [PMID 11443939](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11443939/) | Theoretical interaction with opioids or opioid antagonists |
| `risk:immune-modulation`: tuftsin-derived and alters cytokine expression (inconsistent IL-6 direction) | [PMID 18577961](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18577961/) | Relevance for autoimmune disease or immunosuppression is unknown |
| `risk:nasal-irritation` | Expected for intranasal products; not quantified for Selank specifically | Low-quality evidence |
| `risk:unregulated-product`: grey-market vials | General | The highest practical risk outside Russia |

## Contraindications / special populations
The official Russian label was **not verified**. No pregnancy, lactation or paediatric data were found.

## Hormone axis
**No evidence found** of HPA or HPG suppression; not studied.
