# Weekly digest 2026-W45

> No monitoring body publishes a true weekly list. This digest ranks signals from 2026 reports (UNODC, EUDA, NDEWS, regional alerts). Trend signal ≠ safety.

## Top signals
1. [[bromazolam]] – Dominant designer benzodiazepine in street samples 2024–26, often pressed as fake Xanax or mixed into opioids.
2. [[nitazenes]] – Ultra-potent synthetic opioids, mostly Chinese precursors/synthesis. EU notified 3 new nitazenes + 3 orphines in 2025.
3. [[medetomidine]] – Alpha-2 agonist replacing xylazine as fentanyl adulterant (81% of Toronto fentanyl samples late 2025; 92% xylazine/medetomidine in Philadelphia).
4. [[retatrutide]] – GLP-1/GIP/glucagon triple agonist. The most talked-about grey-market peptide of 2026; most API is made in China.
5. [[alpha-pihp]] – Pyrrolidinophenone cathinone; strong dopamine/noradrenaline reuptake blocker. Among the most-seized cathinones in Europe.
6. [[3-mmc]] – Mephedrone-like empathogenic stimulants; 'chemsex' and party scene.
7. [[cagrilintide]] – Long-acting amylin analog, often stacked with semaglutide in grey-market use.
8. [[tirzepatide]] – GLP-1/GIP dual agonist. Grey-market demand is driven by price.

## Takeaways
- **Weight loss:** tri-agonist [[retatrutide]] and amylin [[cagrilintide]] lead grey-market interest; quality is the main hazard – see [[grey-market-quality]].
- **Wakefulness:** [[flmodafinil]] / [[fladrafinil]] newly flagged as doping substances; no human trials.
- **Stimulants:** cathinones rising in Europe; [[alpha-pihp]] and [[3-mmc]] dominate.
- **Depressants:** [[medetomidine]] + [[nitazenes]] + [[bromazolam]] in the same samples is the deadliest pattern – see [[polydrug-risk]].
