# Depressants timeline, 2024–2026 (newest first)

All dates are as reported by the cited source. Where a source is secondary or the date is inconsistent across sources, it is marked.

## 2026

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **Oct 2026** | Medscape reports **orphine analogues detected across 22 US states**. ([Medscape](https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/potent-orphine-opioids-surge-us-illicit-drug-supply-2026a10011ys)) |
| **14 Oct 2026** | DEA **withdraws its 7 July 2026 notice of intent** on 7-OH and publishes a **replacement notice** in the Federal Register. ([FR](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/14/2026-20943/schedules-of-controlled-substances-temporary-placement-of-7--hydroxymitragynine-and-mitragynine)) |
| **8 Oct 2026** | **HHS letter to DEA** recommending a revised 7-OH concentration threshold. |
| **26 Aug 2026** | DEA **finalises temporary scheduling of mitragynine pseudoindoxyl, MGM-15 and MGM-16** (to 26 Aug 2028). (Federal Register, 26 Aug 2026 — ⚠️ **document number unverified**: 2026-17380 is the FDA/ECDD notice, Docket FDA-2026-N-9208, not this DEA order) |
| **7 Jul 2026** | DEA **notice of intent** to schedule 7-OH above a concentration threshold (reported as >0.050% or >1.0 mg/article). Later withdrawn. |
| **22 Jun 2026** | Philadelphia DPH advisory: **medetomidine in 90% of Q1 2026 samples where fentanyl was the primary drug**. ([PDPH HAN](https://hip.phila.gov/document/6386/PDPH-HAN-MWD-06.22.2026.pdf/)) |
| **18 Jun 2026** | STAT reports CDC data: **nitazene-confirmed overdose deaths 27 (2020) → 409 (2024)**; **>1,100 via SUDORS**; expert estimate up to **~2,000** since 2019. ([STAT](https://www.statnews.com/2026/06/18/nitazenes-deadly-synthetic-opioids-rapid-spread-united-states/)) |
| **Jun 2026** | **EUDA European Drug Report 2026** published: **50 NPS first notified in 2025**; **7 new synthetic opioids (3 nitazenes, 3 orphines)**; **40 new benzodiazepines 2007–2025, 28 on the market in 2024**. ([EUDA](https://www.euda.europa.eu/publications/european-drug-report/2026/new-psychoactive-substances_en)) |
| **29 May 2026** | CND scheduling decisions take effect: **N-pyrrolidino isotonitazene** and **N-desethyl etonitazene** into **Schedule I of the 1961 Convention** (decided at CND 69th session, March 2026). ([UNODC LSS](https://www.unodc.org/LSS/Announcement/Details/475042d4-534c-4a06-b956-4531d4a72432)) |
| **May 2026** | UNODC: **cychlorphine** μ-opioid potency **exceeding fentanyl**; respiratory/cardiovascular depression in mice at lower doses than fentanyl; **78 post-mortem cases** (USA, UK). ([UNODC LSS](https://www.unodc.org/LSS/Announcement/Details/9403c3d1-12b9-49ac-8604-7da583d38d3b)) |
| **Apr 2026** | **CDC Health Advisory / HAN 00527** on medetomidine; state cascades follow (SC, NE, LA County). CDC: **NFLIS medetomidine reports 2,616 (2024) → 8,233 (2025)**, still **<1% of all NFLIS reports**. ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/han/php/notices/han00527.html)) |
| **Apr 2026** | **CDPH emerging issue brief**: only **small amounts** of medetomidine in SF, San Diego, Inland Empire, LA. ([CDPH](https://www.cdph.ca.gov/Programs/CCDPHP/sapb/CDPH%20Document%20Library/CDPHEmergingIssueBrief_Medetomidine.pdf)) |
| **1 Apr 2026** | **Rhode Island reverses its kratom ban** — reportedly the first state to do so. *(Secondary source; unverified.)* |
| **1 Apr 2026** | **Flubromazolam** reported to become permanently Schedule I (US). *(Secondary source only; **unverified**.)* |
| **18 Mar 2026** | **DEA emergency-schedules bromazolam** into Schedule I. FR notice: **259 overdose cases Apr 2021–Feb 2026**, present in **all 50 states**, likely **under-reported**. ([DEA](https://www.dea.gov/press-releases/2026/03/18/dea-emergency-schedules-bromazolam); [FR](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/03/16/2026-05064/schedules-of-controlled-substances-temporary-placement-of-bromazolam-in-schedule-i)) |
| **5 Mar 2026** | **CDC NVSR 75(1)** published (data 2017–2023): xylazine among top drugs in overdose deaths; **6,033 xylazine-involved deaths**, nearly all with fentanyl. ([NVSR](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/nvsr/nvsr75/nvsr75-01.pdf)) |
| **Spring 2026** | **EUDA begins reviews of cychlorphine and spirochlorphine** to inform whether formal EU risk assessments are required. |
| **Feb 2026** | **Public Health Scotland RADAR alerts**: clonazolam **re-emerging**; medetomidine appearing in drugs sold as heroin and as benzodiazepines. ([PHS](https://publichealthscotland.scot/news/2026/february/phs-urges-caution-following-reports-of-increased-harm-from-changes-in-scotland-s-drug-supply/)) |
| **Feb 2026** | **Colombo Plan / CFSRE orphine health alert** — advises considering orphines when opioid toxicity is clinically evident but toxicology is negative. ([CFSRE PDF](https://www.cfsre.org/images/content/reports/public_alerts/Colombo_Plan_Orphine_Alert_Feb_2026_v3.pdf)) |
| **Feb 2026** | Secondary reporting of **UNODC EWA: 34 nitazene analogues**, ≥37 countries. *(Unverified against primary UNODC listing.)* |
| **Jan 2026** | **PHS RADAR quarterly report**: **clonazolam now the most common street benzodiazepine in Scotland**; warns about ethylbromazolam establishing itself. ([DDN](https://www.drinkanddrugsnews.com/clonazolam-now-scotlands-most-common-street-benzo/)) |
| **2026** | **Ethylbromazolam** characterised: global emergence + GABA-A activity similar to bromazolam. ([Arch Toxicol](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00204-026-04433-9); [PMID 42159737](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42159737/)) |
| **2026** | **N-pyrrolidino protonitazene** identified in suspect tablets at an international mail facility. ([J Forensic Sci](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1556-4029.70185)) |
| **2026** | **5-cyano isotodesnitazene** first detected in street drugs via a harm-reduction/drug-checking collaboration. ([Sci Rep](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-35256-4)) |
| **2026** | **PLOS Medicine** self-controlled case series: gabapentinoid + opioid co-use **+30%** poisoning risk; + benzodiazepine **~2×**. ([PLOS Med](https://journals.plos.org/plosmedicine/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pmed.1005035)) |
| **2026** | **Drugs**: second systematic review update on pregabalin/gabapentin misuse. ([Drugs](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40265-026-02342-w)) |
| **2026** | **MATT conference recap**: **3.5% of 311 non-fentanyl prescription opioid samples** BTMPS-positive, attributed to plastic-container leaching. *(Conference summary; **unverified**.)* ([Axis](https://axisfortox.com/matt-2026-recap-btmps/)) |

## 2025

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **Late 2025** | CFSRE reports **nitazene detections declining** and **orphines replacing them** in US markets. |
| **2025** | **UK coroner/rat study**: nitazene mortality likely **underestimated** — post-mortem concentrations can fall below detection thresholds. ([Clin Toxicol](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/15563650.2025.2601141)) |
| **Jul–Aug 2025** | **Maryland** medetomidine-related overdoses frequently accompanied by withdrawal signs (CDC sentinel data). |
| **Jul 2025** | Multicentre Philadelphia medetomidine-withdrawal study (**n=209**): **ICU 77.5%**, **intubation 20.1%**, **dexmedetomidine infusion 73.7%**, often refractory to standard opioid withdrawal treatment. ([PMID 40747932](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40747932/)) |
| **Jul 2025** | **DHS fact sheet CSAC-25-017** on BTMPS: first seen in illicit supply **June 2024**, now in **≥22 states**. ([DHS](https://www.dhs.gov/sites/default/files/2025-08/25_0814_st_btmps_an_industrial_additive_in_the_illicit_drug_supply_fact_sheet.pdf)) |
| **28 Jul 2025** | **ACMT ToxIC NOSE report #18**: confirmed **7-OH toxicity case**. ([ACMT](https://www.acmt.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NOSE-18-7OH-Mitragynine-FINAL-7_28_2025.pdf)) |
| **29 Jul 2025** | **FDA recommends DEA schedule 7-OH as Schedule I**, targeting concentrated products, not whole-leaf kratom. ([Venable](https://www.venable.com/insights/publications/2025/08/fda-announces-plan-to-restrict-7-oh-opioid)) |
| **Mid-2025 (Jun–Jul)** | **China introduces generic/class-wide scheduling of nitazene analogues** — widely cited as the driver of the subsequent orphine shift. *(Causal link is an inference.)* |
| **May 2025** | **Clinical Toxicology systematic review** of nitazene pharmacology and antagonist action (35 studies): **naloxone remains effective**; prolonged toxicity supports **extended monitoring and repeated dosing**. ([PMID 40422647](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40422647/)) |
| **16 May 2025** | Preprint: mitragynine vs 7-OH have **bidirectional effects on breathing** in rats. *(Not peer-reviewed.)* ([bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.05.16.654392.full.pdf)) |
| **8 May 2025** | **FDA consumer notice on tianeptine** ("gas-station heroin") — sold in convenience stores, gas stations, vape shops, online. ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/health-fraud-scams/new-gas-station-heroin-tianeptine-product-trend)) |
| **Mar 2025** | **Protodesnitazene** first notified to UNODC EWA (Canada), then Australia and UK. ([J Forensic Sci](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1556-4029.70478)) |
| **Apr 2025** | **MMWR Notes from the Field** ×2: suspected medetomidine withdrawal syndrome, **Philadelphia Sep 2024–Jan 2025** ([PMC12045483](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12045483/)) and **severe medetomidine withdrawal, Pittsburgh Oct 2024–Mar 2025** ([PMC12045482](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12045482/)). |
| **2025** | **JAMA** (Shover et al.): BTMPS in the illicit fentanyl supply in **9 US locations**; mean **BTMPS:fentanyl ratio 7.4**; BTMPS-positive samples had **lower fentanyl content (3.1% vs 8.7%)**. ([JAMA](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2829939)) |
| **2025** | **EU Early Warning System**: 7 new synthetic opioids notified — **3 nitazenes + 3 orphines**. |
| **2025** | **Clinical Toxicology** case report: non-fatal overdose predominantly attributable to **cychlorphine**. ([Clin Toxicol](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/15563650.2025.2594070)) |

## 2024

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **Last 4 months of 2024** | Philadelphia: **medetomidine in 72% of illegal opioid samples**; **xylazine falls 98% → 31%** — the substitution event. ([MMWR PMC12045483](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12045483/)) |
| **8 Nov 2024** | **Ethylbromazolam** first detected by **Toronto's Drug Checking Service** — over a year ahead of regulatory notice. ([Arch Toxicol](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00204-026-04433-9)) |
| **Oct 2024 – Mar 2025** | **Pittsburgh** severe medetomidine withdrawal cluster. ([MMWR PMC12045482](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12045482/)) |
| **Jun 2024** | **BTMPS first identified in the illicit drug supply**. ([DHS](https://www.dhs.gov/sites/default/files/2025-08/25_0814_st_btmps_an_industrial_additive_in_the_illicit_drug_supply_fact_sheet.pdf)) |
| **May 2024** | **Chicago medetomidine-involved overdose cluster**; fentanyl in all medetomidine-positive samples. **CFSRE alert**: medetomidine rapidly proliferating across the USA. ([CFSRE](https://www.cfsre.org/images/content/reports/public_alerts/Public_Alert_Medetomidine_052024.pdf)) |
| **2023–2024** | Medetomidine appears **sporadically** alongside fentanyl (Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, Chicago) — the pre-surge baseline. |
