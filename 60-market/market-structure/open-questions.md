# Open questions and contested claims

Up: [[market-structure/index]]

## Claims that are commonly asserted and are NOT established

1. **"China's 2019 fentanyl class control caused the nitazene wave."** No UNODC or EUDA substitution analysis making this causal link was found. The *documented* 2019 displacement was into non-scheduled precursor chemicals and into Mexico-based production, not into nitazene analogues. **Unverified; widely repeated.** By contrast, the EUDA's attribution of the **orphine** rise to China's July 2025 nitazene ban *is* published — making 2025 the better-evidenced example of the same mechanism.
2. **"Removal from FDA Category 2 means a peptide was cleared as safe."** The stated basis was **withdrawn nominations**. It is an administrative consequence, not a safety finding, and it authorises nothing. **Unsupported.**
3. **"PCAC recommended six peptides, so they are compoundable."** PCAC is advisory. As of 2026-11-08 none has been placed on the 503A bulks list, and a citizen petition opposing it is pending.
4. **"Compounded semaglutide/tirzepatide are banned."** Patient-specific 503A compounding on a valid prescription remains possible; what ended was the shortage-driven large-volume pathway.
5. **"Enforcement reduced NPS availability."** Published monitoring shows substitution, not reduction. No dataset found supports a durable availability decline for any NPS class.
6. **"Cathinone seizures are falling."** UNODC notes scheduled cathinones migrated into the amphetamine-type stimulant reporting category — an artefact, not a decline.

## Figures needing primary verification
- **"Counterfeit nitazene tablets rose from 380 (2022) to >50,000 (2024)."** Secondary report of EDR 2026; not confirmed against the EUDA text.
- **The 2026 peptide preprint's sample size.** Accounts give ~6,285 reports, ~6,487 samples, and ~64,787 lab records. The per-class N and endotoxin subset size need reading from the preprint. Authorship also unresolved (Mendias? "Awan"? "Krysia et al."?).
- **PCAC July 2026 vote detail** — per-substance votes and reasoning; verify against FDA meeting materials and transcript.
- **FDA warning letter counts and dates** (seven in March/April 2026; five in August 2026; ~30 telehealth letters in March 2026; ~18 total 2024–26) — all secondary; check FDA's Warning Letters database.
- **CBP Cincinnati interdiction window** — Dec 2025–Mar 2026 vs Dec 2024–Mar 2025. Unresolved.
- **Fifth Circuit tirzepatide ruling, 2026-08-27** — single secondary source.
- **MHRA Northampton operation** — dated both Oct 2025 and May 2026 by different sources.
- **HPRA "30-fold rise" in detained GLP-1 products** — verify against HPRA enforcement statistics.
- **PMID 40983778** ("Outsmarting generic legislation…") — journal, authors and abstract unconfirmed; **pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov did not resolve from this session and would need to be allowed in the session's network settings** to verify.

## Genuine gaps in the published literature

### Measurement
- **No denominator for the grey-market peptide trade.** No published estimate of total volume, user population, or the fraction of shipments intercepted. Everything is seizure-side or testing-side.
- **No parcel-interdiction volume series** for NPS or peptides for 2025–26 in either EU or US published statistics. The most recent EU parcel-relevant series ends in 2021.
- **No peer-reviewed measurement of telehealth prescriber counts** or of compounded-versus-branded share. All available figures are commercial forecasts and mutually inconsistent.
- **No randomly sampled grey-market peptide study.** Every existing dataset is self-selected (submitted to a testing platform) or selected for suspicion (customs seizure). The failure rate among sellers who never submit samples is unknown and structurally unmeasurable by current methods.
- **No published rate of mislabelling for peptides analogous to WEDINOS's 36.6%** — i.e. no national drug-checking service routinely accepts and reports on peptide submissions.

### Surveillance design
- **Should wastewater panels be extended** to nitazenes, cathinone NPS, or peptide markers? Currently all three are outside the EUDA/SCORE panel and nitazenes are off US wastewater lists. What is the feasibility and cost?
- **What is the actual sensitivity/specificity of nitazene test strips across the 22+ analogues?** The 78% analogue-detection figure and the caffeine false-positive need replication, and per-analogue performance is unpublished.
- **How long is the detection lag** from first appearance of a substituted analogue to availability of a certified reference standard and a validated confirmatory method? No systematic measurement of this interval was found, yet it is the central quantity for assessing churn risk.
- **RADARS System's current coverage of NPS and peptides is unclear.** No 2025–26 RADARS output on these markets was located; its subscription model limits independent assessment.
- **Non-fatal nitazene overdose remains an explicit intelligence gap** (Houston HIDTA). Is any jurisdiction building a non-fatal surveillance stream?
- **Post-mortem nitazene degradation** — what is the correction factor? The EUDA states undercounting but no quantified adjustment was found.

### Policy evaluation
- **Has any class-wide control been formally evaluated** for net health effect, rather than for compliance or for substance counts? GAO-21-499 frames the trade-offs but is not an outcome evaluation.
- **Did the GLP-1 compounding wind-down shift demand into the grey market?** Plausible and widely assumed; no monitoring body has published an analysis. A natural-experiment design using 2025 wind-down dates against 2025–26 import interdiction and community-testing volumes appears feasible and has not been done.
- **Does the "research use only" framing measurably reduce or increase harm** relative to either prohibition or regulated supply? No comparative evidence.
- **Why did two Texas pharmacies and a third compounder independently receive endotoxin-contaminated raw material?** FDA identified a shared manufacturer of dietary-supplement-grade glutathione. The upstream raw-material pathway into licensed sterile compounding is the open question with the clearest route to prevention, and no published analysis of it was found.

## What to watch, Nov 2026 onward
- **FDA response to the PSM citizen petition** and whether any of the six PCAC-recommended peptides reaches the 503A list.
- **PCAC meeting before end February 2027** on five further peptides, plus GHK-Cu's reconsideration after removal from Category 1.
- **Finalisation of the proposed 503B exclusion** of semaglutide, tirzepatide and liraglutide.
- **Whether orphines receive EU control**, and what appears outside *that* definition — the next observable iteration of the churn cycle. Cychlorphine and spirochlorphine are already under EU risk assessment.
- **Expiry/extension of the July 2024 temporary nitazene scheduling** (term ran to 2026-07-29) and whether H.R. 5032's class approach advances.
- **Whether the 2026 peptide preprint passes peer review**, and whether its figures survive it.
- **WEDINOS 2026/27 report** — whether the mismatch rate continues its drift upward from 35% → 36.6%.
