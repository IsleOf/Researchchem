# Risks caused by the market structure itself

Up: [[market-structure/index]]

These are risks that exist **because of how the market is organised**, independent of the pharmacology of any particular substance. They would persist even if every compound involved were individually benign.

## 1. `risk:unverified-contents` — the label does not describe the contents
**Evidence:** WEDINOS 2025/26, **36.6% of purchase-intent community samples were not the expected substance**; 36% of diazepam-intent samples contained no benzodiazepine; **43% of cannabinoid-vape samples contained synthetic cannabinoids** ([WEDINOS 2025–26](https://phw.nhs.wales/app/uploads/sites/2/2026/08/WEDINOS-annual-report-2025-to-2026.pdf)). Grey-market peptides: **41.6–71.1% failed** basic purity/dose criteria depending on standard; roughly **1 in 42 vials contained none of the labelled peptide** ([2026 preprint](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202604.1748), not peer-reviewed).

**Why it is structural:** there is no identity-verification step anywhere between synthesis and consumption. Nothing in the distribution chain is accountable for the label.

**Consequences:** unintended substance exposure; unintended polysubstance exposure (UNODC: **38% of NPS post-mortem cases involved ≥2 NPS**); and the consumer's own reasoning about risk is based on a substance they may not be taking — so correct harm-reduction decisions produce incorrect outcomes.

## 2. `risk:dose-unknown` — content per unit is not a fixed quantity
**Evidence:** UK bromazolam tablets, median 0.49 mg but range **0.09–5.4 mg** across submissions, and **1.66–2.41 mg within a single batch of ten** ([Gardner et al. 2026](https://analyticalsciencejournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/dta.70045)). Grey-market peptides: the dominant failure mode is dose accuracy, with **over**-label content more common than under-label (~43–53% more than 10% over label in a commercial platform analysis, unverified).

**Why it is structural:** no unit-dose uniformity testing, no release specification, no batch release.

**Consequence:** titration is meaningless as a safety strategy when between-unit variance exceeds the therapeutic window. Same-batch, same-appearance units are not equivalent. **This is the mechanism by which an experienced consumer is harmed.**

## 3. `risk:no-sterility-data` — injectable products with no pyrogen or sterility assurance
**Evidence:** **~15% of the endotoxin-tested grey-market peptide subset was endotoxin-positive**, and **purity did not predict endotoxin burden (R²<0.01, P=0.75)** (2026 preprint). Even in **licensed** 503A pharmacies, 2026 produced three endotoxin recalls of compounded glutathione — FDA noting two Texas pharmacies obtained raw material **labelled dietary-supplement grade from the same manufacturer** — plus one compounded tirzepatide recall for sterility that could not be assured. Reported adverse events: **fever, chills, severe headache, nausea, vomiting, allergy-like symptoms**; FDA's stated hazard range extends to **anaphylactic shock and death**. Historically, falsified peptides seized in Belgium contained **class 1 elemental impurities, arsenic and lead** ([Janvier et al. 2018](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0039914018306283)).

**Why it is structural:** endotoxin is a pyrogen, not an organism. **Sterility and low endotoxin are independent attributes**, and purity assays measure neither. A certificate of analysis showing high chromatographic purity is silent on pyrogen load — which is exactly what the R²<0.01 result demonstrates empirically.

**Consequence:** the most common document used to reassure a buyer does not address the hazard most likely to send them to hospital. Parenteral route removes every barrier that would mitigate a contaminated oral product.

## 4. `risk:analogue-churn-outpaces-testing` — the market moves faster than the ability to detect it
**Evidence:** UNODC EWA register **>1,500 substances** (Aug 2026), with 688 detected in 2024 alone and 101 first-time. EUDA: 50 NPS first detected in 2025, 1,050 monitored. DEA: **22 nitazenes since 2020**. Each control instrument has been followed by appearance of substances outside its written scope — most cleanly, the EUDA attributing the **orphine** rise to China's July 2025 nitazene ban.

**Detection lag, documented:** routine toxicology screens **do not include nitazenes**, causing widespread undercounting; test strips detect only **78% of analogues** and false-positive on caffeine ([*Lancet Psychiatry* 2026](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanpsy/article/PIIS2215-0366(26)00120-3/fulltext)). Nitazenes **degrade in post-mortem blood** (EDR 2026), so even tested deaths are undercounted. Nitazenes are **not on wastewater surveillance panels** (Houston HIDTA; EUDA/SCORE panel covers five established stimulants plus cannabis). Non-fatal nitazene overdose is an **explicit intelligence gap**.

**Why it is structural:** immunoassays, certified reference standards and interpretation thresholds are all downstream of a substance being known. A newly substituted analogue is therefore invisible to clinical toxicology, to drug checking, to wastewater, and to mortality statistics simultaneously, for a period measured in months to years.

**Consequences:**
- **Absence of data is read as absence of risk** by consumers and sometimes by clinicians.
- Emergency care cannot confirm the agent; antidote and supportive-care decisions proceed blind.
- Cause of death is misattributed, which suppresses the epidemiological signal that would trigger a response.
- Safety information for a substituted analogue is **zero**, every time, regardless of how much was known about its predecessor.

## 5. `risk:mislabelling-as-legal-cover` — the "research use only" framing
**Evidence:** FDA's 2026 warning letters concluded that RUO/"not for human use" labelling does **not** determine intended use, which FDA judged from the whole seller presentation including co-sold reconstitution diluent and on-site dose calculators; products were deemed **unapproved new drugs**.

**Why it is structural:** the disclaimer exists to allocate legal risk away from the seller, not to describe the product. Its presence is therefore **positively correlated** with an absence of pharmaceutical-grade controls: no GMP, no release testing, no stability data, no batch traceability, no recall mechanism, no adverse-event reporting channel. Because the product is nominally not for human use, there is also no pharmacovigilance pathway through which harms can be counted.

**Consequence:** harms in this market are structurally **invisible** to the systems designed to detect them. See also [[market-structure/monitoring-systems]].

## 6. `risk:scheduling-status-misread-as-safety`
Legal status carries no information about potency or toxicity. "Not controlled" means "not yet defined," and in the post-ban cohorts potency has moved in both directions. The same error appears in the policy literature's reception: FDA's April 2026 removal of 12 peptides from Category 2 was a consequence of **withdrawn nominations**, yet was widely reported as a safety clearance. See [[market-structure/peptide-compounding-policy]].

## 7. `risk:channel-shift-unmeasured`
Legal supply channels closing (GLP-1 compounding wind-down 2025, 503B exclusion proposed 2026) coincides with growth in research-peptide imports (CBP: **~5,000 shipments** in >300 master cartons, announced March 2026) and with documented adverse-event concerns in telehealth prescribing. **No monitoring body has published a causal analysis linking these.** The risk is that the population most affected moves into the least-monitored channel without that movement appearing in any dataset — mark the causal claim **unverified** but treat the surveillance gap as real.

## 8. `risk:evidence-asymmetry`
The published data on this market are overwhelmingly generated by (a) seizures, (b) deaths, and (c) self-selected drug-checking or community-testing submissions. All three denominators are biased, and the third is commercially entangled — the main 2026 peptide dataset consists of reports submitted to a commercial testing platform, where sellers with good results have an incentive to submit and publish. The true failure rate for sellers who never test at all is **unmeasured and likely worse**.

## Cross-cutting
The six substance-level risks above (1–3) and the three system-level ones (4, 5, 8) interact: unverified contents plus undetectable analogues plus no pharmacovigilance means that a new harm pattern can run for an extended period before any system registers it. That latency, rather than any individual compound, is the defining health risk of this market structure.
