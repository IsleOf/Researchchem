# Global and regional picture (non-US/EU)

Corrective note: most drug-market literature is US and EU centric because those regions fund the monitoring. Regional pictures below differ substantially in both substances and drivers.

## Forward indicator: the WHO/ECDD scheduling pipeline
A US Federal Register notice of **26 Aug 2026** requests comments ahead of international (WHO ECDD) review for: **clobromazolam (phenazolam), cychlorphine, desalkylgidazepam, ethylbromazolam, etomethazene (5-methyl etodesnitazene), spirochlorphine (R6980), etomidate, medetomidine**. ([Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/26/2026-17380/international-drug-scheduling-single-convention-on-narcotic-drugs-convention-on-psychotropic))

This is the single best *forward* indicator in the repo: it names what is about to receive global control, and independently corroborates the orphine and designer-benzodiazepine detections recorded in [[depressants/designer-benzodiazepines]] and [[depressants/nitazenes]].

## Asia
- **Etomidate in vapes** — the anaesthetic etomidate found in vaping products sold as **"space oil" / "K-pods"** in Hong Kong and Singapore. Significance: (a) a sedative delivered by vape, not injection or pill; (b) a **licensed medicine**, not a designer analogue — no clandestine synthesis required; (c) approved for marketing in the US and not CSA-controlled. Now under international review.
- Methamphetamine dominates; **Myanmar** the main source for East/SE Asia, with **South-West Asia (notably Afghanistan) emerging as a major source since ~2019**.
- Non-medical **tramadol** use and trafficking extended into South-West Asia, Near/Middle East, Central Asia and Transcaucasia.

## Middle East
- **The captagon market collapsed.** The end of the Syrian conflict disrupted production and weakened supply networks; a counterfeit Captagon tablet in Lebanon rose from **$2 to $5**.
- **Substitution to methamphetamine** followed: now among the three most-used drugs in Saudi Arabia, with the increase concentrated among people who previously used captagon. A whole-market class substitution — larger in effect than any single new molecule.

## Africa
- **"Kush"** — emerged in **Sierra Leone, 2022**, spread rapidly across West and Central Africa. **Composition contested:** some sources report synthetic cannabinoids and nitazene opioids; at least one lab analysis found none of these, only acetone and formaldehyde. Treat ingredient claims as unresolved.
- Synthetic cannabinoid seizures rose rapidly in West/Central Africa **2021–2024**, mainly linked to kush supply.
- **Khat** dominates African NPS seizures 2020–24; synthetic NPS use most reported in Southern Africa.
- Non-medical **tramadol** widespread in West, Central and North Africa.

## Americas (outside the US)
- NPS not a primary feature of the regional summary; cocaine and high-potency cannabis strains dominate.

## Global
- **755 NPS circulating in 2024, 118 reported for the first time.**
- **Ketamine** supply and use expanding geographically — flagged as a global rather than regional trend.
- UNODC: NPS are emerging faster; markets transforming via technology and instability.

## Known gap
**No 2026 India-specific NPS data could be located**, despite India appearing in EU data as a source of cathinone precursors and of bulk finished cathinone routed via the Netherlands. This is a gap in the published record, not only in retrieval. See [[precursor-space]].
