# Health-risk timeline, 2024–26

Up: [[health-risks/index]]

Dated safety communications, outbreaks and published clusters relevant to cross-cutting health risks. **Newest first.** Pre-2024 anchor events are listed at the end for reference.

## 2026

**24 Aug 2026 — FDA warning letters to online peptide sellers.** CDER's Office of Unapproved Drugs and Labeling Compliance issued letters to several sellers; products named include ipamorelin, tesamorelin and GLP-1s. FDA held that **"research use only / not for human consumption" disclaimers did not shield the sellers**, because dosing information, injection instructions and dose calculators on the same websites established intended human use. Bacteriostatic water sold alongside was itself treated as an unapproved drug. Notably, the letters did **not** reference the peptides PCAC had recommended for the 503A list a month earlier. ([Sheppard Mullin](https://www.sheppard.com/insights/blogs/fda-balances-peptide-policy-with-enforcement-against-unlawful-peptide-sellers) · [McDermott](https://www.mcdermottlaw.com/?p=50381208)) — *company names and product lists come from law-firm alerts; not verified against FDA's warning-letter database.*

**Jul 2026 — National Consumers League nationwide warning** on self-injecting "research-grade" synthetic peptides, noting that the "for research use only" label is used to sidestep drug-safety regulation. ([NCL](https://nclnet.org/2026/07/))

**23–24 Jul 2026 — FDA Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee (PCAC) votes on seven peptides.** The committee recommended **six of seven** for the 503A bulks list: **BPC-157, KPV, TB-500, MOTS-c, Semax, Epitalon**. Votes were narrow — BPC-157 reportedly **8–6 with one abstention** — and **FDA staff had recommended against** adding them. Epitalon was reportedly voted 7–4 on 24 Jul ([[epitalon/index]]).
- **Status is non-binding.** Adding a substance requires notice-and-comment rulemaking, which had not started for any of the seven. ([Pharmaceutical Executive](https://www.pharmexec.com/view/fda-votes-loosen-restrictions-four-peptides) · [AJMC](https://ajmc.com/view/fda-panel-backs-6-peptides-for-compounding) · [American Med Spa Association](https://www.americanmedspa.org/news/fda-advisory-committee-recommends-adding-six-of-seven-peptides-to-compounding-list/) · [RAPS](https://www.raps.org/resource/fda-advisory-committee-backs-two-controversial-peptides.html))
- **Contested:** one outlier source claims BPC-157 **failed** 6–7. It conflicts with all other coverage; treat as unreliable unless confirmed.
- **Health-risk significance: none directly.** A change in compounding eligibility adds no long-term safety data — see [[health-risks/cancer-and-long-term]].

**Apr 2026 — Preprint on grey-market peptide purity and endotoxin.** Analysis of a large public third-party-certificate dataset: **41.6–71.1% of samples failed basic quality criteria**, **~15% had measurable endotoxin**, and **purity score did not correlate with endotoxin level**. ([preprint](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202604.1748)) — **preprint, not peer-reviewed**; secondary sites quoting "40–75%" misstate it.

**2026 — NYC Health Department HAN advisory on medetomidine withdrawal**, extending the 2025 MMWR findings to a further jurisdiction. ([NYC HAN Advisory 2](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/doh/downloads/pdf/han/advisory/2026/han-advisory-2-medetomidine-withdrawal.pdf))

**2026 — TGA safety advisory on unapproved peptide products** (Australia). Named products include **BPC-157, GHK-Cu, TB-500, retatrutide, CJC-1295**, often injectable and often carrying "research use only" disclaimers. Reported adverse events: **severe allergic reactions, systemic inflammatory response syndrome, generalised hypersensitivity**. A joint TGA/CMO statement additionally flagged **abnormal liver function tests or signs of acute liver injury**. TGA also documented a serious reaction to a product **sold as retatrutide in which testing found undeclared semaglutide and no retatrutide**. ([TGA](https://www.tga.gov.au/node/575382) · [Medical Republic](https://www.medicalrepublic.com.au/emerging-side-effects-of-unapproved-peptides/126627) · [Pharmacy Daily](https://pharmacydaily.com.au/news/peptides-in-tgas-sights/119553)) — *exact advisory date not confirmed against tga.gov.au; secondary sources place it mid-2026.*

**2026 — Xylazine-associated wounds scoping review**, *Advances in Skin & Wound Care* 39(4):E185–E193. Synthesises clinical presentation and management; concludes multidisciplinary care spanning wound care, harm reduction and withdrawal management is required. ([PMID 42007753](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42007753/))

## 2025

**Jun 2025 — EMA PRAC concludes NAION is a "very rare" side effect of semaglutide.** Up to **1 in 10,000** treated people; epidemiological finding of an approximately **two-fold increased risk** of NAION in adults with type 2 diabetes on semaglutide. PRAC recommended **label updates**, advised patients with sudden or rapidly worsening vision loss to contact a doctor without delay, and advised stopping semaglutide if NAION is confirmed. **Novo Nordisk publicly disagreed**, stating the totality of evidence did not suggest a reasonable possibility of a causal relationship. ([Pharmaceutical Journal](https://pharmaceutical-journal.com/article/news/eye-disorder-is-a-very-rare-side-effect-of-weight-loss-drugs-concludes-ema-review) · [TCTMD](https://www.tctmd.com/news/eye-condition-very-rare-side-effect-semaglutide-ema-says))

**1 May 2025 — three MMWR reports on medetomidine, same issue (vol 74, no 15):**
- **74(15):258–265** — *Overdoses Involving Medetomidine Mixed with Opioids — Chicago, Illinois, May 2024.* **12 confirmed, 26 probable, 140 suspected** cases across three West Side hospitals, 11–17 May 2024 — the largest reported confirmed medetomidine-involved overdose cluster. Hypertension 95%, **bradycardia 87%**, altered mental status 84%, pinpoint pupils 84%; most confirmed cases had **no or only partial naloxone response**; **fentanyl in every medetomidine-containing sample and specimen**. doi 10.15585/mmwr.mm7415a1 ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/74/wr/mm7415a1.htm) · [PMC12045484](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12045484/))
- **74(15):266–268** — *Notes from the Field: Suspected Medetomidine Withdrawal Syndrome Among Fentanyl-Exposed Patients — Philadelphia, September 2024–January 2025.* **165 patients** hospitalised with fentanyl withdrawal complicated by **profound autonomic dysfunction** (severe hypertension, tachycardia); complications included **PRES** and **NSTEMI**; resistant to medications effective for fentanyl and xylazine withdrawal; **responded to dexmedetomidine**. **A CDC erratum has been published for this report — check it before quoting figures.** ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/74/wr/mm7415a2.htm) · [PMC12045483](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12045483/))
- **74(15):269–271** — Pittsburgh series, **23 adult patients**, Oct 2024 – Mar 2025. A secondary summary giving "ten cases" is wrong. ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/74/wr/mm7415a3.htm))

**2025 — CDC HAN 00527 on medetomidine** in the illicit drug supply: increased overdose risk and a **severe withdrawal syndrome**. Signs listed: tachycardia >100 bpm, severe hypertension, fluctuating alertness, tremor, chest pain, intractable nausea/vomiting; **onset within hours of last use, peaking 18–36 hours later**. ([CDC HAN 00527](https://www.cdc.gov/han/php/notices/han00527.html)) · Jurisdictional follow-ups: [Philadelphia](https://hip.phila.gov/health-alerts/responding-to-overdose-and-withdrawal-involving-medetomidine), [New Mexico](https://www.nmhealth.org/publication/view/general/9538)

**2025 — GLP-1 heart-rate network meta-analysis** in non-diabetic people with overweight/obesity: semaglutide ~**+3.4 bpm** (95% CI 1.69–5.01), liraglutide ~**+2.4 bpm**, orforglipron 36 mg **+9.29** (95% CI 4.45–13.86), tirzepatide 5 mg **+0.52** (95% CI −2.71 to 3.78) vs placebo. ([PMC12918571](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12918571/))

**2025 — Al-Dulaimi: independent replication of Epitalon telomere effects**, finding telomere lengthening in **breast-cancer cell lines via the ALT pathway** — in vitro. ([doi:10.1007/s10522-025-10315-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10522-025-10315-x))

**2025 — Forzinity (elamipretide/SS-31) approved** for Barth syndrome: the first approved mitochondria-targeted drug, while its "energy"-indication trials (MMPOWER-3, EMBRACE STEMI, ReCLAIM-2) had missed primary endpoints ([[ss-31/index]]). Included as a reminder that approval for one narrow indication is not validation of the marketed claims.

**Jan 2025 — EMA PRAC opens the semaglutide NAION review**, triggered by two observational studies. ([Pharmaceutical Journal](https://pharmaceutical-journal.com/article/news/eye-disorder-is-a-very-rare-side-effect-of-weight-loss-drugs-concludes-ema-review))

## 2024

**2024 — Groenewegen et al., synthetic cathinone cardiotoxicity**, *Cardiovascular Toxicology*: two case reports + Dutch Poisons Information Centre review (2012–2022) + scoping review. **222 single-agent cardiotoxicity cases, 63% involving 3-MMC**; literature review of 40 cases included **cardiac arrest n=28**, VT n=4, STEMI n=2, NSTEMI n=2, cardiomyopathy n=1, myocarditis n=2. Exposure largely self-reported without analytical confirmation. ([PMID 38411851](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38411851/))

**26 Jul 2024 — FDA alert: adverse events from overdosing compounded semaglutide.** Patients in most reports drew **5–20× the intended dose**. Drivers: multidose vials/syringes rather than fixed-dose pens, concentrations varying by compounder, mg↔unit↔mL conversion errors by prescribers, and patients unfamiliar with drawing a dose. Events: nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, syncope, headache, migraine, dehydration, **acute pancreatitis, gallstones**; some hospitalised. FDA noted **prolonged observation may be needed given the ~1-week half-life**. ([via Healio](https://www.healio.com/news/endocrinology/20240909/fda-warns-of-adverse-events-due-to-overdosing-of-compounded-semaglutide) · [Patient Care Online](https://www.patientcareonline.com/view/fda-warns-clinicians-of-adverse-events-due-to-overdosing-of-compounded-semaglutide))

**Jul 2024 — JAMA Ophthalmology: semaglutide and NAION.** Mass Eye and Ear retrospective study: among **194 patients with diabetes on semaglutide, 17 developed NAION within 36 months — 8.9% vs 1.8%** on other glucose-lowering drugs. Single-centre neuro-ophthalmology referral population, so heavily selected; this triggered the EMA review. ([summary](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/nachrichten/156981/EMA-prueft-Risiko-von-Semaglutid-auf-seltene-Augenerkrankung))

**2024 — Many FDA 503A Category-2 removals were nomination withdrawals, not safety clearances** (recorded in [[notable-findings]]). Relevant because the category change is routinely misread as a safety signal.

**2024 — Separate JAMA Ophthalmology finding (University of Toronto): semaglutide and neovascular age-related macular degeneration (nAMD)** in diabetic users — a **different condition from NAION**, frequently conflated with it. ([Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/z/45814068))

## Pre-2024 anchor events

**Feb 2023 — MMWR 72(52–53): bromazolam cluster, Chicago.** Three young adults with **hyperthermia, seizures and myocardial injury** after taking **bromazolam sold as alprazolam**; one developed **status epilepticus despite lorazepam, propofol, levetiracetam and valproic acid**, and persistent coma. Authors noted the constellation was unexpected for a benzodiazepine, that **no signal for hyperthermia, myocardial injury or seizures attributable to bromazolam intoxication existed to date**, and that alternatives included anoxic injury, withdrawal features, or an unmeasured intoxicant. ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/72/wr/mm725253a5.htm))

**2023 — Amaducci et al., JAMA Network Open: naloxone in novel potent opioid vs fentanyl overdoses.** 537 ED patients; novel-potent-opioid group received a significantly higher mean number of in-hospital naloxone boluses (**1.33 vs 0.36**); metonitazene cases averaged **3.0** doses with 2/2 receiving CPR (very small subgroup). ([PMID 37642962](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37642962/))

**Sep 2023 — FDA placed BPC-157 in Category 2** of the 503A bulks review, citing safety, impurities and insufficient human data. *(Secondary sources differ on Sep vs Nov 2023.)* ([PMC12446177](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12446177/))

**May 2021 — FDA final guidance on ANDAs for certain highly purified synthetic peptides** (synthetic glucagon, liraglutide, nesiritide, teriparatide, teduglutide referencing rDNA-origin products), stating that **peptide-related impurities and aggregates may pose immunogenicity risks**. The clearest regulatory articulation of the peptide-impurity immunogenicity problem. ([FDA materials](https://fda.gov/media/166572/download))

**Sep 2012 – Mar 2013 — New England Compounding Center fungal meningitis outbreak.** Contaminated compounded methylprednisolone acetate for epidural injection; *Exserohilum rostratum* predominant. CDC reported 233 cases/15 deaths by mid-Oct 2012 and 48 deaths by 10 Mar 2013; retrospective totals cited are higher (one summary: 798 affected / 64 deaths). **Final totals differ between sources — verify against CDC's final report before quoting.** The largest documented US fungal meningitis outbreak, and the reference case for sterility failure in an injectable. ([CDC HAN archive](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20130216175848/http://www.bt.cdc.gov/HAN/han00327.asp) · [PMC4451895](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4451895))

**2008 — Nass et al., 2-year randomised double-blind MK-677 trial.** Fasting glucose **+0.3 mmol/L (P = 0.015)** with decreased insulin sensitivity; **cortisol +47 nmol/L (P = 0.020)**; fat-free mass increased over 12 months. Still the longest controlled human trial relevant to the GH-secretagogue class. ([PMID 18981485](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18981485/))

**2009 — Rodríguez et al.: first recorded signal of higher skin-ulcer prevalence** in people injecting xylazine-containing substances. The origin of the xylazine-wound literature.

**2006 — CJC-1295 phase 2 halted after a participant died**; causality never established ([[cjc-1295/risks]], `risk:trial-death-unestablished-causality`).

**Update 2026-11-09:** The **FDA now requires a NAION warning on all GLP-1 medications** (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound), following the EU PRAC conclusion of Jun 2025. This closes the regulatory arc described above — and reinforces the point that detecting a rare harm required an approved product, prescription databases, a regulator with compulsion powers and a label to change. None of that machinery exists for grey-market supply.
