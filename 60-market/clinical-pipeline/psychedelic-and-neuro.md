# Psychedelic and neuropsychiatric pipeline — regulatory status 2026 (L1)

Part of [[clinical-pipeline/index]]. Related: [[clinical-pipeline/timeline]], [[clinical-pipeline/risks]], [[clinical-pipeline/open-questions]].

**Bottom line as of 2026-11: no classic psychedelic is approved for any indication, anywhere in the US.** Esketamine is the only dissociative/psychoactive novel agent with a full approval in this space. Three phase 3 programmes (psilocybin, lysergide) have now read out positive, which is the real change since 2024.

---

## Psilocybin — COMP360 (Compass Pathways), treatment-resistant depression

- **COMP005** — first phase 3 readout, **June 2025**. Single-dose COMP360 vs placebo. **Met its primary endpoint**: greater reduction in **MADRS** at week 6. Treatment-emergent AEs through week 26: **84.8%** (active) vs **59.8%** (placebo).
- **COMP006** — second phase 3, reported **February 2026**, **met its primary endpoint**. This made COMP360 the **first classic psychedelic with two positive phase 3 trials**. 26-week data in nearly **600** patients.
- **Durability (post hoc, weaker evidence):** **39%** of COMP006 and **25%** of COMP005 active-arm participants achieved ≥25% MADRS reduction at week 6 and on average maintained it through week 26. These are **post hoc analyses**, not prespecified endpoints.
- **Remission rates** for both phase 3 trials had **not been disclosed** as of the Feb 2026 coverage — a notable omission in a depression programme.
- **Regulatory:** FDA **Breakthrough Therapy designation**, a **rolling NDA submission** (sections reviewed as filed), and a **National Priority Voucher** for TRD. Target of completing the NDA in **Q4 2026** with a possible H1 2027 launch `[sponsor]`. **Not approved.**
- **No NEJM/Lancet/JAMA publication of either phase 3 was found** — the evidence is, as of this date, conference posters and company releases. Independent commentary has questioned how high the comparator bar was (placebo-controlled, with psychological support in both arms, and functional unblinding is unavoidable with an active psychedelic).

## MDMA-assisted therapy — after the 2024 CRL

- **August 2024:** FDA issued a **Complete Response Letter** to Lykos Therapeutics for midomafetamine (MDMA) capsules for PTSD, following a negative advisory committee. FDA **requested at least one additional phase 3 trial**. The CRL, later released publicly, cited **safety concerns, inadequate adverse-event documentation, and trial-design flaws** (including problems around functional unblinding and therapist conduct).
- **Since then:** the sponsor was restructured and renamed — **Lykos Therapeutics → Resilient Pharmaceuticals** (originally MAPS PBC). MAPS confirmed in **August 2026** that the **NDA was resubmitted**.
- One secondary source states the resubmission was made **without a new phase 3 trial**; given FDA's CRL explicitly requested one, this is an important claim and is **unverified** against FDA or company primary documents here. Treat the content of the resubmission as unknown.
- A claimed **April 2026 executive order** directing FDA to issue National Priority Vouchers to psychedelics holding Breakthrough designation appears only in secondary/advocacy coverage and is **unverified**.
- **MDMA remains not approved in the US.**

## LSD — MM120 / DT120 (MindMed → Definium Therapeutics)

The most advanced psychedelic programme by number of positive phase 3 readouts.

- **FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation, March 2024**, for generalized anxiety disorder.
- **Phase 2b (MMED008)**, n=198 adults with GAD (HAM-A ≥20), 22 US sites, Aug 2022–Aug 2023, four dose arms + placebo. **Statistically significant dose–response on HAM-A at week 4** (primary endpoint); the authors concluded a single dose produced dose-dependent anxiety reduction and the results informed phase 3 dose selection. Most AEs mild-to-moderate and confined to dosing day. **JAMA. 2025 Oct 21;334(15):1358–1372, doi 10.1001/jama.2025.13481, PMID 40906494**, NCT05407064. Design commentary: PMC12437882.
- **Voyage** (phase 3, GAD, n=214, US) — **positive topline August 2026** `[sponsor]`.
- **Panorama** (phase 3, GAD, n=245, US + Europe, NCT06809595) — **positive topline September 2026**: statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvement vs placebo on **HAM-A total score at week 12** `[sponsor]`.
- **Emerge** (phase 3, major depressive disorder) — reported positive `[sponsor]`; this makes **three positive phase 3 readouts** for DT120 across GAD and MDD.
- Renamed **DT120 ODT** (orally disintegrating lysergide tartrate), developer now **Definium Therapeutics**. 2026 drug profile: PMID 42848417.
- **Not approved.** Phase 3 results are topline/company-reported; full peer-reviewed reporting is pending.

## Non-hallucinogenic analogues ("neuroplastogens")

The structural bet that the therapeutic effect can be separated from the trip, which would eliminate the monitored-dosing-session cost that makes psilocybin and MDMA therapy expensive.

- A 2026 British Journal of Pharmacology review describes a generation of **non-hallucinogenic 5-HT2A agonists** and argues the evidence suggests therapeutic effects and the psychedelic experience **are separable**. doi 10.1111/bph.70485.
- **No controlled clinical efficacy data have been published** for any non-hallucinogenic analogue as of early 2026. Named programmes (Delix, Enveric EB-003, zalsupindole) are at phase 1 or preclinical; reported phase 1 tolerability without psychotomimetic effects is **company-reported, small, and uncontrolled**.
- Preclinical leads: **tabernanthalog** (simplified ibogaine analogue), **JRT** (LSD analogue, UC Davis — no head-twitch response in mice). Neither has entered human trials per the sources found.
- **The field's central unresolved dispute cuts the other way too:** clinical reports correlate the intensity of the acute psychedelic experience with therapeutic outcome, and a 2026 DMT phase 2a programme stated its drug's efficacy *appears dependent* on the intensity of the acute experience. If that holds, the non-hallucinogenic approach fails by design. **Genuinely open.**

## Ketamine / esketamine — the only approved product in this space

- **Spravato (esketamine) nasal spray approved as monotherapy** for treatment-resistant depression — FDA approval letter dated **17 January 2025**, announced 21 January 2025. First and only monotherapy approval for TRD.
- Basis: phase 4 **TRD4005**, randomised placebo-controlled, 4 weeks. **Remission at day 28: 22.5% vs 7.6%** placebo.
- **Schedule III** controlled substance, available only through the **Spravato REMS** programme (in-clinic administration and post-dose monitoring).
- Racemic ketamine for depression remains **off-label** everywhere; the compounded/telehealth ketamine market operates outside any approved indication. Claims about generic esketamine timing found in this research came only from clinic-affiliated blogs and are **unverified**; no authoritative source located.

## Market-signal observation

The 2024 MDMA CRL and the 2025–26 psilocybin/lysergide phase 3 wins have pushed the grey market in two directions at once: toward compounded/telehealth ketamine (which has an approved analogue to point at) and toward "legal" tryptamine and lysergamide analogues positioned as research chemicals. Note the asymmetry the trials make explicit — every positive phase 3 above delivered the drug in a **monitored session with psychological support and medical screening**. None of that is reproducible outside a clinic, and the trials provide no evidence about unmonitored use. Recorded as a market signal; no sourcing information.
