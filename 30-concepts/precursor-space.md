# Precursor control space (2026)

**Scope note:** this note covers precursor *control policy and monitoring* — what is scheduled, how the regime is evaded as a regulatory phenomenon, and what seizure data show. It deliberately contains **no chemistry**: no routes, no conversions, no mapping of which uncontrolled chemical substitutes for which controlled one. That mapping is the synthesis problem itself, and it is not what a harm-reduction knowledge base is for.

## The three-tier structure
1. **Scheduled precursors** — listed in the 1988 Convention tables. Trade is licensed and tracked.
2. **Designer precursors** — INCB's own term: *"a close chemical relative of a controlled precursor that is purpose-made to circumvent controls."* These have no legitimate industrial use; their existence *is* the evidence of intent.
3. **Pre-precursors** — chemicals one step further back, which convert to a precursor. Legitimate uses are thinner but sometimes real.

## Why this is now the dominant control problem
- **Of the 16 internationally controlled precursors for amphetamine-type stimulants, all 16 are designer precursors.** Of the 12 other internationally controlled chemicals, all are designer precursors too. The control lists have, in effect, become a record of past evasion rather than a description of industrial chemistry.
- **The APAAN cascade is the worked example of futility.** APAAN was placed under international control in 2014. APAA appeared. APAA was controlled. MAPA appeared. Each step took roughly 2–4 years and each produced a chemical with less legitimate use than the last.
- **Scheduling now targets series, not single chemicals.** On 3 Dec 2024 two fentanyl precursors plus **two whole series of closely related designer precursors** came under international control — the precursor-side equivalent of the generic class bans used for the drugs themselves.
- **The EU maintains a separate designer precursor list**, last updated 3 July 2026.

## What this implies
- **Precursor control shifts geography, not volume.** Multiple 2026 sources note China is no longer the only exporter of fentanyl precursor chemicals; India and other jurisdictions feature in EU cathinone supply (48.5 t in 2024 was largely bulk import routed through the Netherlands, with processing concentrated in Poland).
- **The lag is structural and roughly symmetrical** with the drug side: a new designer precursor takes years to schedule, during which it is legal to trade.
- **Seizure statistics measure enforcement attention as much as market size** — 2.6 t of cathinone precursors seized in the EU in 2024 alongside 63 dismantled production sites.
- **For risk, the relevance is indirect but real:** precursor substitution changes the impurity profile of the final product. Residual process impurities are one of the documented harms in unregulated injectables and are invisible to the purity certificates sold with grey-market product.
