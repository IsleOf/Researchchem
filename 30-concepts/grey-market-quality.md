# Grey-market peptide quality

Independent testing (2026 preprint) found wide variation in purity and dose; older studies found 10–90% less active ingredient than labelled. Endotoxin contamination causes fevers and ER visits. 'Research use only' labels mean no regulator checks anything.

## Applies to
[[retatrutide]] · [[tirzepatide]] · [[cagrilintide]]
