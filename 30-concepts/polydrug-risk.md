# Polydrug risk (depressant stacking)

Most deaths involve combinations: opioid + benzo + alpha-2 sedative + alcohol. Naloxone reverses only the opioid part – still give it and call emergency services. Don't use alone.

## Applies to
[[nitazenes]] · [[bromazolam]] · [[medetomidine]] · [[alpha-pihp]]
